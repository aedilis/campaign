The Merope System is a formerly Imperial-aligned system within the Caldraeus Cluster which has been claimed by the a group of mutant rebels.

# Background

The Merope System was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence on any of its worlds.

&nbsp;

Settlements were always sparse, as the two terrestrial planets were small and ill-suited to growth. However, the gas giant [Merope](#/maps/merope) contains atmospheric chemicals that can be processed into promethium cheaply, and so harvesting and refinery infrastructure has long existed on Merope's moon [Radaghast](#/maps/radaghast). Some time before the end of M41, the Imperium set up a penal colony on Radaghast that compelled its prisoners to mine Merope and ship its product to the rest of the cluster.

# M42

In 020.M42, a group of alien cultists calling themselves the [Cousinry of the Broken Chain](#/factions/cousinry-of-the-broken-chain) had infiltrated the prisoners and a number of Imperial staff, and overthrew the brutal overseers in a bloody uprising. From there, they quickly took control of the other planets in the system. While this uprising is often triggered by the proximity of a Hive Fleet, this was not the case for Radaghast; rather, the cult had grown quickly in the oppressive conditions of the prison world, and they became strong enough to defeat the oversociety without waiting for Ascension Day.

&nbsp;

Unknown to most members of the cult, there was another reason for this premature victory; the system's frequent exports allowed them to smuggle agents throughout the rest of the cluster, spreading word to other societies that their faction could triumph over the seemingly-invulnerable Imperium. Imperial agents quickly registered the Merope System as compromised and ordered all imports seized and destroyed at planets receiving them; it is unknown which planets may have received cargo before this warning went out.
