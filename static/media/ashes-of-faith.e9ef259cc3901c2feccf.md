# Into the Dark

...whispers in the tunnels...

...murmurs in the mind...

For months now, rumors have spread through the Hab-bunkers of Telemane that a new power is to arise. Following this lead, Inquisitorial agents of the Crimson Laurel diligently tracked down the occult practitioners of this Heretical sect before they could make their public display. In the private halls of the civic underbelly, the Emperor's detectives rooted out the vile Apostates. This root, however, proved deeper than expected.

The Galleries Below had several rooms for the forces to clash in, made all the more deadly by their close quarters. Though the Imperial troops seized ground early, they were taken aback by the swift riposte caused by the hideous mutations of the Chaos Devotees.

Had their foe had any faith or courage, the Crimson Laurel would have easily dispatched all enemies with pistol power and las fusillade. Resorting to profane betrayal, the Congregation of the Whisperer compelled the Inquisitors to garrison above and leave the underworld in the hands of the Dark Gods... for now...

#### +++Chaos Victory+++

# The Cult Revealed

With the underhive secured, the cultists burst into the streets revealing themselves to the hive in full. The Crimson Laurel met the Congregation of the Whisperer once more in the streets surrounding the Military Intelligence Headquarters. Amidst the buildings, gantries and haze that was rolling in, operatives fired and fought attempting to interrogate the opposition to learn vital information.

Making quick use of the clear line of sight before the smoke came, the Cult Demagogue fell to a direct blast from a melta cannon. A veteran guardsman held the flank facing charge after charge of mutated Devotees, securing vital intel long enough to bring it back before being gunned down. The smoke rolls in and all are blinded, cult blades are drawn and cut into Imperial flesh as the melee begins. In a wild frenzy of combat both sides take heavy losses by the time the panicked crowds rush past, and the Arbites clear the area. While the Cult disappeared into the masses, the Crimson Laurel came out on top, having learned of the cult's next move to claim the power generators in the deep city...

# Deadly Hangar

After the fight in the open streets, the agents of the Crimson Laurel had discovered that the Congregation of the Whisperer planned to make a move to seize vital power cores in an abandoned hangar in the Catacombs of the Righteous. Bringing with them a compliment of the Sisters of Silence to the close confines below, power must be controlled to control the Power of the hive.

Moving cautiously, both the Agents and the Cult began to slink into the darkness of the hangar, and flank around the halls surrounding it. Footsteps and murmurs echoed in the dark eerie silence. Lights flared and the horrid mutations of the dark came charging forward under a hail of fire from readied agents. Torments burst around the apparatus and fell upon a Sister preoccupied with a hangar door, only to fall to a krak grenade immediately after. The Hangar itself was a bloodbath of shots being fired, flesh mutating, and corrupted weapons clashing with Imperial steel. Each time a Sister fought she would hack grievous wounds in the cultists but their foul mutations proved too strong, and each Vigilator in turn was overwhelmed by the onslaught. The fighting was too sudden for the Agents to take and were forced to pull back, securing a single core, but the Cultists were left with many more primed to power their foul rituals.

# Steal the Ciphers

The Congregation of the Whisperer has all the power they need now, but still the defenses of the hive still slow them. Without the brute strength to take full sections of the hive, yet, they will need to bypass Imperial security, and that means capturing ciphers that will allow them passage throughout the hive unobstructed. The Crimson Laurel is waiting there to meet them and protect the vital codes in the Refinery of His Wondrous Might.

The alchemite stacks of the refinery belch out toxic smog that obscures and chokes the area, all the Agents could do is dig in and train their weapons on the ominous clouds waiting for the dark figures to emerge. Emerge they did, torments thunder out of the smoke and crash into guardsman killing the demolition expert instantly, they swarm over the platforms and gantries, cutting off the valves of smog to allow access to the the cogitators storing the ciphers. With most of the smog gone the Agents open fire, a hail of shots from the Veterans and the Agents drop many cultists in a concentrated barrage, but distance was lost and the cultists themselves fired with pistols and witchery. Operatives fell fast and often on both sides. The Agents were able to hold off the cult long enough that even the single cipher they took wasn't able to make up the difference. While the Iconarch was hit with a plasma blast, dying instantly, enough of their insidious schemes were enacted that they came out on top, even without being able to secure the ciphers.

#### +++Imperial Victory+++

# Conclusion

Having stopped the Congregation of the Whisperer from completing their Dark Ritual, the agents of the Crimson Laurel rest assured that they have forestalled the total corruption of Telemane. Forced to confront the calamitous infection of cults springing across the world, soon enough even the devoted of Telemane begin to hear the Voices. Though the Imperium can claim control of the hab-bunkers, the bastions, and manufactorums, no longer can they look up to the sky and say that sun and stars shine upon an Imperial world.
