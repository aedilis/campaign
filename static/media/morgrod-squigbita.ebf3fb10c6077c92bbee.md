Freebooter among the [Bilge Squigz](#/factions/bilge-squigz) and first mate of its splinter krew, [da Badskwal Bukkaneerz](#/factions/badskwal-bukkaneerz), under [Kaptin Grogbreff](#/characters/gobsmakk-grogbreff). His name, missing eye, and handsome hair squig all relate to the same night of too much fungus brew, but Squigbita thinks the eyepatch only serves to make him look more proppa.

# Invasion of Caldraeus

Squigbita was first mate aboard *Da Bad Skwall*, a boarding ship within [Urkrod Blakkjaw's](#/characters/urkrod-blakkjaw) invasion fleet. After their defeat, his ship crashed on the human industrial world [Eryx](#/maps/eryx), though Squigbita survived and joined Grogbreff's raiding party while stranded on the planet.
