In their relentless drive to purge the galaxy of the witch, the heretic, the mutant, the Black Templars under the command of Hochmeister-Regent Eisenberg found their way into Caldraeus on a Purgation Quest against the denizens of an unstable Space Hulk. Though their forces may be battered and initially with limited resources, Duty sustains their eternal zeal and restoration imperative. While Eisenberg rebuilds and extracts their Battle Barge *The Aegis of Iron* from the ruins of the space hulk, much of the command of the Revenant Crusade in Caldraeus has been delegated to [Marshal Reinhardt von Rosenherz](#/characters/reinhardt-von-rosenherz) to persecute a ruthless cleansing campaign upon this unclean sector of warp-tainted space.

# Background

Taken from the broken remains of both the Vengeance Crusade of Andilar, and the Templars of the Valinor Crusade, Eisenberg commands the Black Templar forces in Caldraeus. Originally planning to return to the Valinor Crusade along the Angels Argent in repentance for failing to save their Brothers' homeworld, Eisenberg's fleet was steered off course. Marshal Reinhardt, ever the hot-head and brashness being his lifelong vice, careened the *Aegis of Iron* toward the space hulk [*Void Phantom*](#/maps/void-phantom). Eisenberg, at the time debriefing his officers on the war plan, was too late to change course of his flagship. What seemed at first to be a blind rage against a fledgling vanguard xenos force turned instead into a more critical threat, as the space hulk held a [Starfort](#/maps/mournful-sepulchre), and untold scores of [the Archenemy](#/factions/plaguefester-warband). Seeing this oversight as a new front in the Eternal Crusade, Eisenberg committed to the attack. Soon after, the whole force was swallowed into the warp.

&nbsp;

Now in the Caldraeus Cluster, Eisenberg has donned the title Hochmeister-Regent, and has set his wounded band to reestablishing a new Revenant Crusade in these isolated stars. More out of penance than trust at this point, Eisenberg has appointed Reinhardt as the Marshal of this crusade: to form a foothold in the Cluster while Eisenberg musters the core of their strength.
