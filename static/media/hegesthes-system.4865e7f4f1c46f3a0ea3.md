The Hegesthes System is an Imperial-aligned system within the Caldraeus Cluster. As with all Imperial systems in the cluster, it falls under the jurisdiction of the Ecclesiarchy-aligned government of [Caldraeus](#/maps/caldraeus). Specific system control is determined by the government of its most populous planet [Hegesthes](#/maps/hegesthes), with a high degree of autonomy among its planets.

# Background

The Hegesthes System was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence on any of its worlds.

&nbsp;

Worlds are defended by local PDF as well as better-trained regiments from Hegesthes, colloquially known as [Hegesthean Dusters](#/factions/hegesthean-dusters).

# M42

In 016.M42, the system was invaded by Ork [Bilge Squigz](#/factions/bilge-squigz) freebooters under [Kaptin Blakkjaw](#/characters/urkrod-blakkjaw). They were quickly defeated by [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox) and the [Fleet of the Amber Cloud](#/factions/fleet-of-the-amber-cloud) in a battle that destroyed the Ork capital ship, the *Skrap Throne*, and caused the presumed death of its top warboss. Many Ork ships were destroyed, but a few surviving transports and roks fell to the planets within the Hegesthes System, bringing Xenos invaders to the defenders of each world.

&nbsp;

Fortunately for the Imperium, the Orks have been highly disorganized since the loss of their leader, and the majority of their time is spent fighting each other instead of the humans. In this way, while Orks have been an ever-present threat for the planets of the system since the invasion, they have not stood a real chance of conquering any of the Imperial worlds. Defenders remain vigilant lest a suitably charismatic Ork take the place of Blakkjaw and reunite the fractured Waaagh!
