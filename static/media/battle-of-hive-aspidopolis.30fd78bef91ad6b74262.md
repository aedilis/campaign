Following the [calamity in the Underhives](#/events/ashes-of-faith), the forces of Chaos have only grown throughout Telemane. Some mysterious dispatch from the Sector Three Transport Hub made its way to one of the Noble Houses' Fortress Spires, but with no apparent sabatoge. Settling in for a long campaign of habitation to habitation purgation, the Telemane Defense Force could hardly expect the next assault to take shape so soon.

Dusk soon settles over the great bastion Hive Aspidopolis. Billions of souls within its depths rely on the circuit of reinforced walls that surround the spire. Atop these fortifications, the Telemane Defense Force spies dust clouds on the horizon. Surely the Knight Houses have roused themselves to aid in the garrison! Yet as the auspex clears, and the machines of war close in, twisted metal and corrupted sigils adorn the chassis of this fell host.

High Command demands that a force led by Castellan Adamo take a combined arms force into the ruins at the feet of the bastion. There, they can secure any abandoned ordinance the enemy might use against them, and perhaps stymie the foe's strength before it can test the walls.

- ![](11_battle_of_hive_aspidopolis/01-02.jpg "")
- ![](11_battle_of_hive_aspidopolis/01-03.jpg "")

Auspex signs go silent, and simmers in the dim dusk indicate motion on the Traitor lines, though no discernable action can be detected. In such a way does positioning take on the appearance of lurking terror.

A signature maneuver of Telemane's Finest, Adamo commands that all motorized units advance on No Man's Land. Sure enough, the TDF secures all ordinance. Volatile as they are, it becomes clear they must hurl these dangerous explosives into the enemy lines.

- ![](11_battle_of_hive_aspidopolis/02-01.jpg "")
- ![](11_battle_of_hive_aspidopolis/02-02.jpg "")
- ![](11_battle_of_hive_aspidopolis/02-03.jpg "")

The first opening salvoes are devastating and terrible. Biding their time paid off, as the Chaos Knights launch promethium, bolt, and beam into the core of the armoured ranks. Battle Tanks explode, and whole units are turned into cinders as the house of Doom demolishes the Defense Force vanguard.

The riposte comes in the form of a Tempest! Dropping behind enemy lines, the elite troops establish a locus of control in the Chaos Knights rear. The strong arm of superior numbers ensures that the ordinance never reaches too close to the Defense Force's lines of command.

- ![](11_battle_of_hive_aspidopolis/03-01.jpg "")
- ![](11_battle_of_hive_aspidopolis/03-02.jpg "")

The tide begins to turn in the middle of the battle. Though the defenders of Hive Aspidopolis have a hold on the objective, they are losing ground and manpower. The Knights of Doom crash through the strong garrison and pulverize the stalwart Bullgryns. For a brief moment, there are traitors everywhere.

Panic does not set in for those with faith in the Emperor. Castellan Adamo recognizes the great push as directed by powerful Lords of Chaos thrashing their thralls. Adamo orders a concerted effort to bring down the enemy commanders. The War Dog Stalker, foul Lieutenant to the terrible *Umbra Mortis*, is laid low by the raging volleys of Righteous Retribution. With one of the Traitor leaders down, the Defense Force is primed to exploit this gap in both command and firepower.

- ![](11_battle_of_hive_aspidopolis/04-01.jpg "")
- ![](11_battle_of_hive_aspidopolis/04-02.jpg "")

The battle progresses with speed. The remaining Knights hold the center of the warzone with unparalleled dominion. Furthermore, this allows them to return the dangerous ordinance back toward the Militarum's lines.

Retribution is swift for the Guard have objectives secured once more, snatching them from the hands of Heretics. At last, the end seems close, and the Imperium has a vast advantage in casting the unexploded ordinance into the enemy ranks.

Tragedy strikes at the last, however, as the remaining Knights of House Mordgier and Dreadblade Umbra Mortis dig in on the center field. Seemingly abandoning their primary objective, House Mordgier focuses instead on making an unbreakable bulwark in the middle of the killing field. Most of their guns turn to obliterating Militarum infantry, and other units adept at securing objectives. In a last ditch effort to deny this turn of action, the brave soldiers of the Telemane Defense Force attempt to wrench control from the Traitors. Just barely surviving an attack to the north, the War Dog Brigand holds against Armoured Sentinel and Scions alike, while the Perfidious Egregor strikes into the backline command. Whether a display of composure or a fit of fury at seeing his quarry once more, [Commissar Schoenfeld](#/characters/commissar-schoenfeld) faces against the Umbra Mortis alone, charging a defiant plasma blast directly at its mask... to no avail.

- ![](11_battle_of_hive_aspidopolis/05-01.jpg "")

Retreating back into the bastion around Hive Aspidopolis, the Telemane Defense Force can only look on in horror as the Traitor House Mordgier snuffs out points of resistance to their fell betrayal. Though it was a close call that came down to a few chance circumstances, Telemane is now securely a Traitor planet with enclaves of resistance.
