Former Flash Gitz kaptin and survivor of the early Hegesthean wars, now serving as his own warboss on [Hegesthes](#/maps/hegesthes) with the assistance of his vulcha squig Hornswoggul.

# Beginnings as Flash Git

Kaptin Skumdreg first led a small krew of Flash Gitz known as da Bragga Boyz, working with [Mek Lugnutz](#/characters/lugnutz) in the earliest parts of the Hegesthes War. After a long-running siege with little progress to show on either side but massive casualties, most of the Orks besides Lugnutz and Skumdreg had died or wandered off. Lugnutz eventually joined up with the ill-fated Waaagh! Ghurrok, while Skumdreg sought to rebuild his flashy krew. However, now surrounded by squabbling boyz on a world of skrap and sand and humies rather than flashy dakka-lovers aboard a proppa Orky fleet, Skumdreg found fewer Bragga Boyz in search of plunder and more greenskins in search of a boss with a plan. Not one to shy from an opportunity for more loot, even if it looked differently from how he expected, Skumdreg knocked enough heads to assemble a following of his own.

# A Growing Waaagh!

Skumdreg's rise corresponded with a massing of Orks along the humie border, following the successes of emboldened [humie defenders](#/factions/hegesthean-dusters) and organizing work of a [strangely inspired weirdboy](#/characters/wortskab).
