Kalymnia is an Ork-held world in the [Kalymnia System](#/maps/kalymnia-system).

# Background

Kalymnia is believed by Administratum officials to be a dormant Necron tomb world. Previously, there was a light Imperial presence for monitoring and research, but occupation of the planet was always treated highly cautiously.

# M41

In the 700s M41, as the nearby Hybraxis Nebula of the Maelstrom Zone faced the threat of a large Ork Waaagh!, xenos pillagers spilled into the Caldraeus Cluster as well. Though the Orks were eventually defeated, they had thoroughly overrun the Kalymnia, [Serres](#/maps/serres-system), and [Tybar](#/maps/tybar-system) Systems, and the Imperium strategically withdrew from all three.

# M42

The Orks have taken great satisfaction in breaking and looting the old structures that dot the landscape and extend far beneath its surface. As the easy-to-reach loot dries up, Orks have been venturing further and further into the strange catacombs.
