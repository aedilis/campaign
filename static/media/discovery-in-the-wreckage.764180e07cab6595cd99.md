Ever since wreckage from the *Mournful Sepulchre* made planetfall along with its traitor and loyalist survivors, the local Orks have been gleefully tearing through the debris for scrap and fighting each other over the best loot. Meanwhile, both of the Astartes kill teams have slowly regrouped, getting their bearings on the alien world and reconnecting while taking out what isolated xenos they can.

Finally, after months of scrapping, one band of Orks inadvertently activated an array that sent out a curious signal. While it meant nothing to the greenskins themselves, its trace was quickly picked up by the more advanced communications equipment of both the [Marshal's Mules](#/factions/marshals-mules) and the [Phyletic Vector](#/factions/phyletic-vector), and each realized that salvaging enough parts from the array could be the key to rebuilding a network that would allow them to get back in contact with their parent units. Each approaches the wreckage from different angles, wary of each other's presence and cautious not to draw the attention of an overwhelming number of xenos eager for new foes.

## The Marshal's Mules
#### Stealth Offensive
###

Under cover of darkness, the Marshal's Mules advance on the Ork compound as the idle grot sentries fiddle aimlessly with their spotlight. As the Mules line up in perfect formation against the ramshackle walls of the camp, [Sergeant Engelbert](#/characters/sergeant-engelbert) gives a curt order and his team opens fire.

The Black Templars are the picture of their perfect training. Disciplined bolter fire fells Ork after Ork on the spot, as the meager return fire bounces harmlessly off their armor. Orks brandishing crude weaponry leap the barricades and attempt to close the distance, but the marines are protected by their ideal positioning as they continue to savage the xenos.

The few Ork survivors at last close the gap, and Engelbert and Tarcuin are waiting for them in melee. Finally, the Orkish obsession with brutal melee pays off, and as the fighters gang up on what they clearly recognize as the boss, Engelbert is brought lower and lower by rains of blows from Ork boyz and one extraordinarily bold gretchin. In the end, Engelbert buckles under the blows of the final survivor; in the same instant, Jan raises a bolt rifle to avenge his fallen commander, and doesn't stop firing until the Ork is an unrecognizable smear in the dirt.

With the field empty, the Mules fan out to assert their full control of the compound and extract all that they require. Engelbert finds his feet, and his team has plenty of time to make an extensive survey before disappearing again ahead of the next Ork patrol.

## The Phyletic Vector
#### Infiltrate the Landing Pad
###

Across a jagged stretch of badlands, the Death Guard of the Phyletic Vector conduct a different sort of infiltration. Diminutive sentries meander around an Orkoid compound as the Astartes steadily approach the bunker from which their signal emanates. Hulking transhumans move deceptively silently across the scrapyard and await their fellows behind heaps of rubble while the grots shirk their duty, until one hapless creature rounds a corner and comes face to face with [Myasum Clostrid](#/characters/myasum-clostrid), Gulchor, and Malignus.

Deathskulls spill out of the brew hut they had been carousing in as the remaining Death Guard cast stealth aside and storm forth. A rokkit hits Festus behind a barricade and explodes in a shower of viscera as the marine goes down. His teammates return fire and take down the two big gunners and two sentries as the fighters cross the field.

The Orks close to swinging distance as the first marines reach their target bunker. The Deathskull Nob bellows a challenge as he charges Myasum Clostrid, power klaw versus power fist. The Ork's first swing connects, though the plague champion's disgustingly resilient flesh sloughs off a surprising amount of damage. Myasum Clostrid swings his fist back into the xenos champion, cracking his skull and sending him down along with all the blight launcher damage he had taken on the way in.

The Death Guard leader bears down on the bunker, and is jumped by one more Ork fighter who manages a few deep stabs before Clostrid crushes him in his fist as well. Fairly injured now, he lurches closer to his destination, keeping pace with Gulchor as they breach the outer walls. Just as they do so, Orks atop the bunker descend upon them screaming battle cries. One falls upon Gulchor, seriously injuring the warrior. Clostrid leaps to his ally's aid, smashing into the xenos with his power fist, but the return choppa stab is finally enough to send the plague champion careening back into darkness.

Another boy descends on the wounded Gulchor as his commander's body hits the floor. Even injured, Gulchor is a formidable foe, and easily dispatches the new arrival before turning on the first boy, who charged him and killed his commander. He slashes with a plague knife and takes a choppa blow in return, but as he thrusts a blow that should kill the enemy, its tough Ork physiology seems to shrug off the blow entirely, and its choppa flashes through a gap in Gulchor's armor as his body joins his lord's on the dusty ground.

The Ork luck can only hold for so long, and the rest of the Phyletic Vector finish putting down the remainder of the beasts across the battlefield. The last injured Ork charges Malignus, but with barely a breath of life left in him after taking down two Astartes warriors, he falls before the foul marine's riposte. No xenos are left on the field as the surviving Death Guard close in; they seize some salvage from the scrapyard and recover their fallen comrades, but have lost far too much time to achieve their ends here before the position will become overrun, and retreat back to their stronghold.

# Conclusion

The transhuman teams make off with some salvaged goods from the wreck of the hulk, and with this new signal data, perhaps the greatest lead they've had at reconvening with their comrades since first landing on this hostile world.

The Marshal's Mules took their recovered assets to a secured cave bunker they had constructed, expanding their armoury and setting up a war shrine for stronger contemplation after their battles. They added a rare targeting ocular they had seized from the wreck to their arsenal. Jan, in particular, felt his experience had led to a superior battlefield role.

The Phyletic Vector returned to their plagued wreckage, fully recovered. While they lacked sufficient time to establish a secure presence closer to the main crash site for the critical signal technology, they successfully steered the Ork patrol away from a profane site they had left behind. Their long-term vision of a heretical ritual bringing Nurgle's presence more strongly to this planet is one step closer.
