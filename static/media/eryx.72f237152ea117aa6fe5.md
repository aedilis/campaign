Eryx is an Imperial industrial world in the [Hegesthes System](#/maps/hegesthes-system).

# Background

Eryx was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence here.

# M42

In 016.M42, after a group of [Ork freebooters](#/factions/bilge-squigz) were defeated by [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox), surviving Xenos landed or crashed on planets throughout the Hegesthes System, including Eryx. Defenders immediately engaged the threat, and so far, have been able to keep it largely contained.

&nbsp;

In 022.M42, after [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox) survived [Zaldrotha Voidrender's](#/characters/zaldrotha-voidrender) ambush, he raised the alarm throughout the cluster and allowed time to rally defenses. Eryx was able to marshal its defenses effectively, and when the [Doom Fleet](#/factions/the-doom-fleet) attempted to expand from the [Belgatha System](#/maps/belgatha-system) via seizing Eryx's production centers, the loyalists [defeated the initial invasion](#/events/knights-vs-chaos). The Voidrender was deeply displeased with the original Night Lords commander, and replaced him with [Lord Vargouille](#/characters/lord-vargouille), who [encountered the local Ork population](#/events/chaos-orks-clash-eryx) and attempted to manipulate their numbers to break the Imperial defenses.
