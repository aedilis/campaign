A veteran of the Valinor Crusade, Ulrich rose from the ranks of Marshal Eisenberg's fleet through the tumult of Tyrannic invasion of that violent sub-sector.

## The Vengeance Crusade

Alongside the Angels Argent chapter, Ulrich had no shortage of foes to share the glory with in the face of the Biotide of the Great Devourer. By the time Eisenberg and other Marshals were called away to engage in the Pariah Nexus, Ulrich was granted a post as Castellan.

&nbsp;

Ulrich's prowess and dedication to martial honor took off during the campaign in the Pariah Nexus known to outsiders as the *Tears of Andilar*, the Vengeance Crusade to the Templars who gave their blood to the cause. Castellan Ulrich excelled in the close quarters combat common in the Andilar Cluster. Ulrich proved his vow to Abhor the Witch by felling the dire sorcerer Erevos Shadowtongue on Yspen Secundus, he Suffered Not the Unclean when he smashed the first and last infection of the Genestealer's Clawed Spire on Lachrimae, and Upheld the Honour of the Emperor by guiding Ultramarines to the Astronomicon's light, but most excellently did Ulrich display his reverence of his vows to Accept Any Challenge, No Matter the Odds in his campaigns against Necron Overlord Tynekh the Builder. The mechanical xenos suffered a great defeat in the ruins of Khortep, the concentrated conclusion of Ulrich's first Oathsworn Campaign. Reveling in their leader's supreme mastery of war, the Vengeance Crusade elected that Ulrich shed the markings of Castellan, and rise their new Marshal.

&nbsp;

While there were setbacks by the end of the *Tears of Andilar*, Ulrich squandered the chance for war not. Tynekh, in single combat, severed Ulrich's right arm. Thousand Sons from the Cult of the Shattered Sky agitated the Warp Storm: *Malodium's Corridor* to cut off support from the wider Imperium. In the face of this darkness, Ulrich led his troops to glory, raising neophytes to the ranks of initiates, striking down the armies of the Xenos Dynasties, and navigating his Strike Cruiser to obliterate the vile blackstone construction the *Phylactery of Bones* to detonate in *Malodium's Corridor*. Though they could not save the Homeworld of their erstwhile brothers, the Vengeance Crusade saved the Imperium from the corruption of that Cluster via the leadership of Marshal Ulrich and his Mules.

## The Void Phantom

Clearly caught off guard and reeling from the loss of Andilar to the Warp, Marshal Ulrich is currently missing. Sent [on a mission](#/events/slaughter-on-sublevel-185) deep into the *Mournful Sepulchre* by Hochmeister-Regent Eisenberg, Executive Officers of the Revenant Crusade in Caldraeus have yet to receive word from any marine who embarked on that mission.
