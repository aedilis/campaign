Telemane is a formerly Imperial-aligned planet in the [Belgatha System](#/maps/belgatha-system). It was the seat of a number of loyalist knightly houses before the [invasion of the Doom Fleet](#/events/doom-fleet-invades).

# Background

Telemane was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence here.

# M42

In 022.M42, after [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox) survived [Zaldrotha Voidrender's](#/characters/zaldrotha-voidrender) ambush, he raised the alarm throughout the cluster and allowed time to rally defenses. Telemane was able to marshal its defenses effectively, and when the Voidrender eventually made planetfall, the loyalists [beat them back](#/events/defense-of-telemane) in the first defeat the Night Lords experienced in the cluster under their Warmaster.

&nbsp;

Following this loss, the Voidrender began [a long campaign](#/events/ashes-of-faith) to undermine the Imperial defense from within. This campaign failed its ultimate goal of performing a profane ritual to pull Telemane into the influence of the Warp, but it did spread enough Chaos corruption throughout the planet that the Imperial defenders were forced to gather in the holdout Hive Aspidopolis for a desperate last stand. Although the [final battle](#/events/battle-of-hive-aspidopolis) was extremely close, the Night Lords were victorious thanks to a combination of Zaldrotha's tactics and his corruption of key knightly houses.

&nbsp;

The defense of Hive Aspidopolis bought the loyalists enough time to evacuate key resources and personnel before they could be lost to further Chaos corruption. As a result, the Night Lords hold mostly uncontested control of the plnaet, though pockets of resistance still exist and survivors work hard to stop the Great Enemy's plan.
