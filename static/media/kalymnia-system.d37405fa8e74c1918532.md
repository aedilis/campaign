The Kalymnia System is a system within the Caldraeus Cluster.

Formerly it served as the seat of a Necron dynasty It has been occupied by Orks since the 700s M41. It consists of two planets orbiting the star Tybar, officially designated [Tybar Primus](#/maps/tybar-primus) and [Tybar Secundus](#/maps/tybar-secundus), though each is conventionally referred to as Tybar as well.

# Background

[Kalymnia](#/maps/kalymnia) is believed to be the former seat of a regional Necron dynasty that controlled the Kalymnia System along with the adjacent [Serres System](#/maps/serres-system) and [Tybar System](#/maps/tybar-system).

&nbsp;

After the dormancy of the Necrons, it appears to have gone unoccupied for many millennia, until the push to finally colonize the Maelstrom Zone led humans to the system in M39. However, the world was fairly inhospitable, and the tomb structures are apparent enough that the Imperium kept their presence to a controlled minimum.

&nbsp;

In the 700s M41, as the nearby Hybraxis Nebula of the Maelstrom Zone faced the threat of a large Ork Waaagh!, xenos pillagers spilled into the Caldraeus Cluster as well. Though the Orks were eventually defeated, they had thoroughly overrun the Kalymnia, [Serres](#/maps/serres-system), and [Tybar](#/maps/tybar-system) Systems, and the Imperium strategically withdrew from all three.
