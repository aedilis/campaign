Although victory for a Genestealer Cult is often the same as its destruction on Ascension Day, there are occasions when the cult achieves a revolutionary victory early, perhaps out of necessity upon being discovered or by achieving supremacy in arms earlier than expected. So it was for the group styling itself the Cousinry of the Broken Chain, in which prisoners overthrew their cruel guards and liberated the entire [Merope System](#/maps/merope-system). They have continued to survive as the sparsely-settled planets outside the moon colony are below the Imperium's notice, and they cannot spare the forces to obliterate the central Radaghast body. The cult persists, in control of its world with its members believing they have achieved the freedom they sought, and hoping the agents they sent abroad will find equal success throughout the sector.

# Background

It is not known if the first infected human arrived on the prison moon of [Radaghast](#/maps/radaghast) as a prisoner themselves, or smuggled aboard a supply drop, or amidst the crew of a convoy picking up the promethium that the chain gangs had harvested from its gas giant [Merope](#/maps/merope). What is known is that sympathy for the cult spread like wildfire among the rampantly mistreated prisoners and even lower Imperial staff, and that when the administrators and wardens finally discovered what had been going on under their noses, they were far too late. The uprising was swift and its victory was total.

&nbsp;

While the Adeptus Administratum would eventually realize the world had fallen under enemy control and enact a strict embargo of the regular shipments that its prison labor had provided much of the surrounding area, it is not clear for how long the members of the cult were able to ship their tainted cargo to ports accustomed to receiving them. Undoubtedly the cult has sent agents to many other planets within the system, and it is up to each Planetary Governor to wipe out any emergent threats to their citizenry with extreme prejudice.
