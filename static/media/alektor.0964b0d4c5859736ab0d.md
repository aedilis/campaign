Alektor is a formerly Imperial world in the [Omadikea System](#/maps/omadikea-system).

# Background

Alektor was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence here.

# M42

After the arrival of the [Daybreak Protectorate](#/factions/daybreak-protectorate) and its union with the Omadikea System, Alektor saw the settlement of T'au along with their new Gue'vesa allies, but most prominently became the new home of the Enclave's sizeable population of Kroot auxiliaries. The jungle world is fairly similar to their home world of Pech, and the xenos quickly filled the portions of the planet too dense or humid for previous human population centers.
