One of the most highly engaged squadrons of Imperial pilots in the Gandymere Air War against the [Orks](#/factions/squigbuzza-skallywagz), these flying aces have been on the front lines since the fighting first began. Decorated pilot [Decker Bask](#/characters/decker-bask) secured unprecedented numbers of kills in his Thunderbolt early in the war, playing a decisive role in the long period of Imperial domination of the skies.
