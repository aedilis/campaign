Ialaranth is an Aeldari maiden world in the Caldraeus Cluster.

# Background

Imperial records show little of the history of the planet, but it can be surmised that it survived the Fall of the Aeldari and is jealously protected by its Exodite occupants. Any attempt at Imperial investigation or expansion has been met with extremely violent preemptive strikes by [a group of Craftworld Aeldari](#/factions/viridian-weavers). As the planet does not occupy a particularly important position, its conquest was deemed a waste of resources.
