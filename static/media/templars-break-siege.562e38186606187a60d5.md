*Silence. Most of the siege was filled with silence. Despite the ruins and host of xenos surrounding the hive, despite the wounded and destruction, despite the explosions and weapons fire ... hardly a word was ever spoken.*

*St. Holden's Hive had been surrounded months ago, or maybe longer. The initial Ork attacks devastated the city: grand architecture fell to crude bombs, clean air was choked by acrid smoke, pristine streets were slick with ash and greasy rain. Day by day, week by week the attacks and skirmishes whittled away at the garrison. Each tank lost was added to the ruins, each trooper that died was left to bleach in the sun. The city was surrounded; no help was coming. Most of the thoroughfares had been blocked or destroyed to limit the ability of large scale invasions to enter the city but a few supplyways remained intact. Here the defense was strongest and the fighting fiercest, and ever vigilant eyes kept constant lookout for the next probing strike. Swift reaction of the officers and warning of approaching Orks kept the city mostly clear of Greenskins. Then the dark clouds gathered, slowly approaching the hive, dark as the doom it brought. The sun was blotted out plunging the hive into darkness; cutting rains dropped visibility to a few hundred meters; the war camps and movements of the Orks disappeared from view. St. Holden's Hive was blinded, and the roar of engines and tramp of heavy boots went unheard and unreported.*

Lieutenant Gravish peered through the port of his Leman Russ *Valiant*, past the rain streaks washing the grime from the demolisher cannon's barrel, into the dark stretch of Supplyway Alpha 9. A thunderous crack and flash of lightning sent a static jolt through the onboard Auspex, shutting it down. The storm has persisted for two days with no sign of letting up, but at least he had the steel shell of *Valiant* whereas Captain Vorne was with the scouts, up the supplyway, camped out in those sodden ruins. He kept peering. In his gut he knew and attack was coming; the Orks wouldn't pass up on this darkness to make a surprise attack. The engines rumbled idly and the storm pattered constantly but there was a heavy silence. He heard no sounds from the platoon in the ruins—just over fifty troopers hiding out—no chatter from the Vox connecting him to the other Leman Russ *Spearhead*, the squadron of Sentinels, or the pool of Tauroxes that were all charged with the defense of Supplyway Alpha 9.

The sky lit red with a burning flare suddenly shot into the sky, and hot beams of lasfire erupted en masse from the ruins half a kilometer down the road. Harsh barking shots and muzzle flashes replied. Hundreds of flashes front-lit the charging Greenskins running through the ruins along side of the road. The Vox burst into life as Gravish ordered the Tauroxes forward to reinforce and the Sentinels to wheel around to flank the attack with their heavy flamers. He quickly spotted on the range finder and called it out to *Spearhead* which rewarded his call with a boom of cannon fire, the explosive shell blowing apart the remains of a wall and spraying the air with broken green bodies. Gravish gritted his teeth, and *Valiant* rumbled forward to join the battle.

Gravish wiped his brow with a cloth stained with the last five hours of sweat. The battle had gone well for the first three, but it changed when the air battle started. Unseen in the dark clouds, the rain was joined by a hail of bullets from above as the Aeronautica engaged the flying Orks, peppering the ground and forcing the troops to give ground in order to take cover. He had himself witnessed the smoking Valkyrie that leveled the administratum center, where half of Vorne's men had dug in, as it crashed, and the cut off Vox told him the Tauroxes and their crew were smoldering wrecks. He hadn't heard back from the Sentinels in hours. They were in a brief lull in fighting but he could see in the flash of lighting the distant shape of Ork transports blitzing down the roadway, and the skies were on fire with the renewed air battle, though Gravish thought the Aeronautica had been defeated some time ago. One Sentinel broke into view returning finally, but alone. As he reached for the Vox set for a report the broken auspex burst into life with a wailing of danger. Threat level Terminus pulsed on its screen and a shadow blotted out all of the little light from above.

An enormous flying fortress bore down on Alpha 9, its guns bright as suns as a hailstorm of large caliber rain saturated the area. Bullets ripped through *Valiant*'s hull, shattering the spotlight and killing his Vox master instantly. The newly arrived Sentinel didn't have time to take cover and took a full blow. He saw it teeter but keep its feet, lit from behind by the explosive light of crashing planes. Vorne's position took heavy fire but his unit was out of sight. The familiar silhouettes of dakkajets flitted overhead but the shape of Thunderbolts were absent. Instead aircraft he didn't recognize flew in and out of view at speed.

Gravish didn't have time to dwell on the mystery craft as the Ork assault started once more. Masses of Orks ran on foot and packed transports skirted between the broken remains of buildings. *Valiant* and *Spearhead* advanced and with an expert spotting Gravish guided the two cannons to aim true, their combined fire demolishing the vehicles only for the Orks inside to clamber out and start running forward. Behind the usual shape of Orks, Gravish made out lumbering hulks of armor making haste toward Vorne's position.

A gout of burning promethium from the Sentinel's heavy flamer was cut short and the sounds of Ork warcries drowned the death screams of troopers on the other side of the road. More of the Ork 'trukks' were approaching from down the road picking up foot sloggers as the demolisher cannon spun around toward the north, blasting over a dozen Orks apart with its dreadful impact. Still they came swarming across the rubble, and behind them a horrible machine of clanking metal and saws was rattling closer. Bursting from the south, more Orks butchered the guardsmen that were escorting the tanks. There was no doubt Vorne wouldn't have let them through if he had still lived. Crude axes and whining power klaws attempted to hack and peel away the armor of *Valiant* as they began to fully encircle Gravish. He readied his laspitol, determined to take the head of at least the first Ork that breached the hatch before he died as they began swarming over the tank, the hulking armored Orks approaching ever closer with armor-cutting saws glinting through the night.

**"KILL THE ALIEN! CULL THE MUTANT!"**

The booming machine voice cut through the sounds of battle as swiftly as the lascannon fire disintegrating the orks on his hull.

**"NO PITY! NO REMORSE! NO FEAR!"**

The voice echoed through the hull. Gravish clapped his hands over his ears protectively, and his eyes found the auspex which read:

*Imperial Signal Detected — Signature:*

+ASTARTES+

*Valiant* reversed at full power as Gravish flung open the hatch to the cupola. The rain stung his face as he rose. A behemoth of black armor stomped closer from behind. A slit of blazing red, like the Imperium's own fury, illuminated the honors and sigils adorning the immense hulk: laurels, crosses, parchments, and a double-headed Aquila. Gravish clutched at his own Aquila pendant and offered a prayer to the Throne of Terra for His mercy.

"The Emperor's Angels! We are delivered!" *Spearhead* called out through the Vox. Armored forms, in executioner's black, came charging out of the ruins to the south. Gravish caught a glimpse of a massive sword blacker than the Void lifted on high by the leader, before they disappeared into the ruins.

The Orks surrounding him were blasted apart by the stalwart Dreadnought. Gravish immediately barked orders into the Vox and at his crew; they would not be founding wanting in the eyes of the Angels. The two tanks unleashed a barrage of fire into the hordes that were slowly taking control of Alpha 9. Alien corpses were blasted apart under the disciplined attack. The Auspex pinged and a mass of signatures, enemy and Astartes, flared in the ruins. Gravish watched both sides lose markers until all enemy lights were snuffed out.

Just as he brought his sight up, a hulk of pistons and smoking metal rushed *Valiant*. A whirlwind of saw blades and fire washed over the tank as the Leman Russ' steel shell was shredded and chewed apart underneath the onslaught. The blades were just carving into the crew compartment less than a foot away from Gravish when two lascannon bolts pierced through the Ork walker, destroying its foul heart. The Dreadnought stood triumphant, its twin lascannon shoulder red with heat.

**"NONE SHALL LIVE THAT OPPOSE THE IMPERIUM!"**

It spoke before continuing on its way, firing at the Orks on the road that were now in retreat, heading away from the Hive.
