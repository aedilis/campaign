Badlandz, once known to Imperial records as Serres, is an Ork-held world in the [Serres System](#/maps/serres-system).

# Background

The Serres System appears to have been within the domain of an ancient Necron dynasty centered on [Kalymnia](#/maps/kalymnia). Little is known about what became of this dynasty, and whether Badlandz would have been a significant location to them.

# M41

In the 700s M41, as the nearby Hybraxis Nebula of the Maelstrom Zone faced the threat of a large Ork Waaagh!, xenos pillagers spilled into the Caldraeus Cluster as well. Though the Orks were eventually defeated, they had thoroughly overrun the Serres, [Tybar](#/maps/tybar-system), and [Kalymnia](#/maps/kalymnia-system) Systems, and the Imperium strategically withdrew from all three.

# M42

No other factions have appeared to take interest in Badlandz, so the Orks who occupy the planet have taken to merrily fighting each other.
