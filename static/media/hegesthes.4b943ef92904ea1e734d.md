Hegesthes is an Imperial-aligned planet in the [Hegesthes System](#/maps/hegesthes-system). As the largest planet in the system, it exerts the most geopolitical influence on its neighbors, though it is ultimately accountable to the Ecclesiarchy-aligned government of [Caldraeus](#/maps/caldraeus).

# Background

Hegesthes was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence here.

&nbsp;

Worlds are defended by local PDF as well as better-trained regiments from Hegesthes, colloquially known as [Hegesthean Dusters](#/factions/hegesthean-dusters).

# M42

In 016.M42, after a group of [Ork freebooters](#/factions/bilge-squigz) were defeated by [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox), surviving Xenos landed or crashed on planets throughout the Hegesthes System, including Hegesthes. Defenders immediately engaged the threat, and so far, have been able to keep it largely contained.

&nbsp;

Hegesthes received the most Xenos invaders within the system, and has seen the most action in the last few years. This has manifested in a long stalemate, with years-long campaigns resulting in very little territorial exchange. As the local regiments train more and more against the disorganized Orks, their fighting proficiency has increased, and lately the war has turned slightly in favor of the Imperium, though the Ork response seems to be rising to the occasion.
