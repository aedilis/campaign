Commissar Schoenfeld is one of the most notably heroic defenders of [Telemane](#/maps/telemane), being present for the Imperium's first defeat of [Zaldrotha Voidrender](#/characters/zaldrotha-voidrender) and holding the line nearly long enough to claim victory in the final battle.

# The Telemane War

When the [Doom Fleet](#/factions/the-doom-fleet) first invaded Telemane, it was with a record of unstoppable successes in the [Belgatha System](#/maps/belgatha-system), and all seemed hopeless. However, the Telemane Defense Forces spent every conceivable effort preparing their defense, and Schoenfeld was present for the initial battle in which the loyalists [held the line](#/events/defense-of-telemane) so heroically that it ground the Night Lords' progress to a halt for months.

&nbsp;

Over the course of much of the next year, the forces of Chaos led an [insidious campaign](#/events/ashes-of-faith) to undermine Imperial control and morale in strategic locations throughout the planet. While this was largely successful, Schoenfeld was present at the Imperial stronghold of Hive Aspidopolis, which resisted Chaos corruption. This would become the location of the [final battle](#/events/battle-of-hive-aspidopolis) for control of Telemane, which the Voidrender won extraordinarily closely. In one of the most inspiring moments of the planetary defense, Schoenfeld charged the corrupted [*Umbra Mortis*](#/characters/umbra-mortis) alone, firing a plasma blast at it that for a brief moment looked as though it could have turned the tide.

&nbsp;

Despite the traitor victory, the near-draw of the final battle allowed for an organized Imperial evacuation of critical resources and personnel. Schoenfeld survived and was one of the top priorities for bringing off planet, given his experience fighting the Great Enemy, and was relocated to the next front where the Emperor needed him most.

# The Eryxian War

The industrial world of [Eryx](#/maps/eryx) in the Hegesthes System became the next focal point of the war with the Night Lords, as the [Hegesthean Dusters](#/factions/hegesthean-dusters) sought every advantage they could muster in expelling the warband under [Lord Vargouille](#/characters/reshig-vargouille) while dealing with the planet's [ongoing headaches](#/factions/bilge-squigz).
