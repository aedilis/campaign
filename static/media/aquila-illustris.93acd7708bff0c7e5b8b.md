The *Aquila Illustris* is the Emperor Class flagship of the [Fleet of the Amber Cloud](#/factions/fleet-of-the-amber-cloud), commanded by [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox).

# Background

As the foremost flagship of the Caldraeus navy, it is likely the *Aquila Illustris* played a role in the defeat of [Kaptin Blakkjaw](#/characters/urkrod-blakkjaw) in 016.M42.

# The Doom Fleet Invasion

The *Aquila Illustris* was present at the [Doom Fleet ambush](#/events/doom-fleet-invades) of 022.M42. While the battle was a sound Imperial defeat, the *Aquila Illustris* was able to destroy the Murder Class Cruiser *Drach'nyen's Tremor* and survive long enough to retreat and raise the warning throughout the cluster, resulting in prepared defenses to counter the Night Lords' first assaults on [Eryx](#/events/knights-vs-chaos) and [Telemane](#/events/defense-of-telemane).

&nbsp;

It was eventually reinforced by frigates from its original fleet that had been salvaged before the [attack on Naxos](#/events/naxos-besieged). With this greater strength, it returned to the [Belgatha System](#/maps/belgatha-system) to aid in the evacuation of critical resources from [Telemane](#/maps/telemane) after it fell in a [climactic battle](#/events/battle-of-hive-aspidopolis).
