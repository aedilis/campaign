The Tybar System is a formerly Imperial-aligned system within the Caldraeus Cluster. It has been occupied by Orks since the 700s M41. It consists of two planets orbiting the star Tybar, officially designated [Tybar Primus](#/maps/tybar-primus) and [Tybar Secundus](#/maps/tybar-secundus), though each is conventionally referred to as Tybar as well.

# Background

The Tybar System was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is believed to have been the territory of a Necron dynasty centered on [Kalymnia](#/maps/kalymnia) previously, though little is known about their disappearance.

&nbsp;

In the 700s M41, as the nearby Hybraxis Nebula of the Maelstrom Zone faced the threat of a large Ork Waaagh!, xenos pillagers spilled into the Caldraeus Cluster as well. Though the Orks were eventually defeated, they had thoroughly overrun the Tybar, [Serres](#/maps/serres-system), and [Kalymnia](#/maps/kalymnia-system) Systems, and the Imperium strategically withdrew from all three.

# M42

In late 023.M42, the [*Void Phantom*](#/maps/void-phantom) Space Hulk appeared within the system. [Activity onboard the hulk](#/events/escape-the-mournful-sepulchre) led to the ejection the [*Mournful Sepulchre*](#/maps/mournful-sepulchre) Starfort from the Hulk, causing pieces of the structure to rain down upon the surface of Tybar Secundus. With the *Mournful Sepulchre* now orbiting Tybar and the *Void Phantom* nearby, many more factions seem poised to take an interest in this system.
