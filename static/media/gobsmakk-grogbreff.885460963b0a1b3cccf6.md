Freebooter kaptin among the [Bilge Squigz](#/factions/bilge-squigz) and boss of its splinter krew, [da Badskwal Bukkaneerz](#/factions/badskwal-bukkaneerz). A former Deathskull before turning pirate and roving with the Bilge Squigz, Grogbreff is a freebooter through-and-through whose original clan allegiance shows up in his prized blue coat and claim of proppa Deathskull luck persisting into his piratical career.

# Invasion of Caldraeus

Grogbreff was kaptin of *Da Bad Skwall*, a boarding ship within [Urkrod Blakkjaw's](#/characters/urkrod-blakkjaw) invasion fleet, along with his first mate [Morgrod Squigbita](#/characters/morgrod-squigbita). After their defeat, his ship crashed on the human industrial world [Eryx](#/maps/eryx), and Grogbreff formed a raiding party along with his surviving krew to pillage local settlements.

# Eryx Crusade

Grogbreff first became a notable threat to the Imperium after he crossed paths with [Gorgob Gitstompa](#/characters/gorgob-gitstompa), the warboss of another band of stranded Orks. Gitstompa told him about the Chaos warband he had [fought earlier](#/events/chaos-orks-clash-eryx) but been unable to find since. Intrigued by a new enemy, the Kaptin offered the boss a spot on his krew; though Gitstompa didn't relish playing second fiddle, he had even less taste for continuing his fruitless search for the slippery new enemy, and so he accepted the offer.
