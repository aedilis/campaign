Naxos is a formerly Imperial-aligned planet in the [Belgatha System](#/maps/belgatha-system). The planet itself is largely uninhabitable, but orbital stations around it provide key logistics and material support to the Imperial fleet.

# Background

Naxos was settled some time before it become uninhabited. Its satellite stations have long played a role in its system's importance for safe fleet activity near the Maelstrom.

# M42

In 022.M42, after the [invasion of the Doom Fleet](#/events/doom-fleet-invades), Night Lords Warmaster [Zaldrotha Voidrender](#/characters/zaldrotha-voidrender) sought to establish control of the entire system. The crew of the Naxos orbitals had responded to the [Fleet of the Amber Cloud's](#/factions/fleet-of-the-amber-cloud) defeat by pulling the wrecks of damaged frigates into repair distance and operating tirelessly to restore them to fighting capacity. The Night Lords agent [Lord Malithor](#/characters/lord-malithor) attacked the stations under Zaldrotha's orders, seeking to claim its advantages for himself in a [campaign he ultimately won](#/events/naxos-besieged). The loyalists of Naxos dispatched the frigates they could repair, along with supplies and personnel, back to [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox) in the hope that their sacrifice would give the Imperium some advantage in the long war.

&nbsp;

Malithor secured his victory over the superior numbers and area knowledge of the Imperials by detonating a Virus Bomb that killed everyone aboard the station. He and his retinue then took over operations, attempting to make the system work for their fleet in the long run.
