Lugnutz occupied the unusual distinction of leading a small warband as a Big Mek instead of a as a warboss. He was active on the locked lines of Hegesthes against the [local humies](#/factions/hegesthean-dusters) for years, leading a siege on the city of Jahannam Tertius in 018.M41 that caused widespread destruction but led to no territory changes.

# Joining a Growing Waaagh!

By early 023.M42, Lugnutz had joined up with a promising new warband under the warboss Ghurrok, seeming to hope that embedding himself in a more traditional structure could be the key to a breakthrough. Unfortunately for him, this growing Waaagh! attracted the attention of the 317th Imperial Regiment, which prioritized eliminating it before it could fully develop. After a [dedicated aerial assault](#/hegesthes-bombing-run), the Third Company under [Captain Kyser](#/characters/captain-kyser) invaded and gunned down every Ork in the camp in [a one-sided fight](#/events/hegesthes-camp-raid).

&nbsp;

Lugnutz has not been seen since, though another possible explanation is that his beloved kustom force field appears to have gone out of fashion for big meks some time that year.
