Kathikos is a formerly Imperial-aligned planet in the [Kathikos System](#/maps/kathikos-system). However, in recent years it has declared its independence from the Imperium and is now treated as being in open rebellion.

# Background

Kathikos was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence on any of its worlds.

# M42

The human government of Kathikos, long frustrated by contributing so many resources to the defense of a cluster that they rarely saw return for, resolved to stop paying their Imperial Tithe. They aim to exist as an independent system, but Water Caste envoys from the [Daybreak Protectorate](#/factions/daybreak-protectorate) of the nearby [Omadikea System](#/maps/omadikea-system) are making overtures to the planetary governor about joining their enclave. The governor has been deliberately unclear in their responses, as the Imperium of the Caldraeus Cluster have also sent soldiers to put down the rebellion, and the governor hopes to use the help of the T'au to defeat them without committing to submitting to them after.
