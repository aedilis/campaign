The warhost serving the interests of the Aeldari in Caldraeus space uses the webway to traverse between the maiden worlds it protects or reclaims and any strategic location as it explores the region's widespread Warp access in search of artifacts that may offer power or information they can use in their total war against Chaos.

# Background

Before the Fall, the Aeldari maintained a presence throughout what is now known as the Caldraeus Cluster. While many worlds were lost or abandoned in the aftermath of the catastrophe, the presence of exodites on a number of maiden worlds ensured that there remained at least some outposts of civilizations past those dark days.

&nbsp;

Defending these outposts from the raiders and expansionists of the region is reason enough for the interest of a warhost, but the proximity of this territory to the worlds lost in the Maelstrom means there is an even greater opportunity to recover what was lost. In particular, some warriors of Craftworld Iybraesil have shown a commitment to the region, as part of their tireless search for the spirit stones and lost relics of the ancient empire within the Crone Worlds claimed by the Warp. While the craftworld itself remains situated near the Eye of Terror elsewhere in the galaxy, a contingent of warriors known as the Viridian Weavers have utilized webway travel to monitor, defend, and occasionally expand their territory here, in what was before the Great Rift the second largest access point to the Warp.

# Activity in Caldraeus

While the webway allows them near limitless travel to wherever they need to appear, there are still limitations, and the Weavers maintain alliances with Exodite Aeldari, such as those on [Ialaranth](#/maps/ialaranth), as well as neutral Aeldari pirates of the region, such as the Ebon Krakens and the Stygian Kestrels.
