The Serres System is a formerly Imperial-aligned system within the Caldraeus Cluster. It has been occupied by Orks since the 700s M41. 

# Background

The Tybar System was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is believed to have been the territory of a Necron dynasty centered on [Kalymnia](#/maps/kalymnia) previously, though little is known about their disappearance.

# M41

In the 700s M41, as the nearby Hybraxis Nebula of the Maelstrom Zone faced the threat of a large Ork Waaagh!, xenos pillagers spilled into the Caldraeus Cluster as well. Though the Orks were eventually defeated, they had thoroughly overrun the Serres, [Tybar](#/maps/tybar-system), and [Kalymnia](#/maps/serres-system) Systems, and the Imperium strategically withdrew from all three.
