The [Hegesthean 317th Regiment](#/factions/hegesthean-dusters) had been making strong progress in reclaiming territory following [Captain Kyser's](#/characters/captain-kyser) [victory over Warboss Ghurrok](#/events/hegesthes-camp-raid). His tactics of smaller strike forces taking key locations proved very effective. But due to the nature of such spread-out combat, it made the attention of the Orks look to more heavily fortified areas for a fight. Warbands swept through Imperial lines in a tide of destruction, looting and setting up scrap towns out of the ruins of outposts. Captain Kyser left [Lieutenant Raphe](#/characters/lieutenant-raphe) and Fourth Platoon to return and lead the counter offensive to slow the spread of the Orks.

Meeting in the ruins of the outpost Kyser leads his troopers on the attack coming in from the east in the hope of watching the Orks off guard. Both sides ready for battle, intent on purging their foes.

The 317 begins on the offensive, rushing to take ground. Veteran troopers make an armored push and deploy their teleport homers in the middle of the battlefield with their Chimera staying as support. Munitions begin to fly but while wounding one of the Ork trukks nothing is destroyed. A burst of warp energy erupts in the North West corner as a [Weirdboy](#/characters/wortskab) accidentally kills four of his bodyguards, fire from the trukk downs a Veteran and their medic begins work on him. Flash Gitz pile out of their transport and set their sights on the southern armored sentinel. Under a withering hail of fire from their snazzgunz the armored sentinel barely holds fast through the onslaught.

- ![](09_hegesthes_border_raid/01-01.jpg "The Imperial gunline let their munitions fly against the Xenos")
- ![](09_hegesthes_border_raid/02-01.jpg "Flash Gitz greet the Sentinel with a blistering volley of dakka")

Understanding that the warp energy is a sign the Orks are trying to teleport into his lines, Captain Kyser spreads out his escort of guardsmen to cover the eastern objective and as much of their rear line as possible. On the southern front Bullgryns attempt to charge into the Flash Gitz but don't have the momentum to clear the ruins; the wounded sentinel charges them instead and bypasses the overwatch and kills one of the Ork shooters. On the northern front, between fire from the armored sentinel and battle tank the second trukk is destroyed, and a blast from the hellhound's flame cannons thin out the survivors. Attempting to finish the job, it charges in but between the nob's power klaw and the vicious attacks from the Warboss the hellhound is brought low to within an inch of destruction. Tempestus Scions drop in the Ork backline and attempt to delay the Weirdboy but only kill a few of the bodyguard. With all fronts being engaged and the central area being denied, the Orks ready themselves for their WAAAGH!. The Weirdboy successfully teleports his bodyguard to the northeastern flank, in cover, and puts out some small arms fire on the sentinel. More Orks pour in from reserves but are not able to reach the scions quite yet. A Deff Dread rushes in from the southern edge and burns most of a heavy weapons squad with its skorcha. The Warlord Warboss and his boyz disembark from their trukk to make their way through the ruins to get to the center of the battlefield. There they charge in, bursting out of the ruined building and strip the hull of the Chimera to all but nothing in their surprise attack. The trukk then moves in to support the Flash Gitz against the sentinel. On the northern side the warboss and nob are able to destroy the hellhound; after bringing it down they turn their klaws toward the center objective and the veterans.

- ![](09_hegesthes_border_raid/03-01.jpg "The elite Scions drop into the thick of the enemy back rank")
- ![](09_hegesthes_border_raid/03-02.jpg "The Hellhound burns away most of the infantry and closes in to finish the job")
- ![](09_hegesthes_border_raid/03-03.jpg "A hulking Deff Dread noisily enters the fray")

Being outflanked by the Deff Dread, the Leman Russ is forced to turn all of its weapons upon it and is able to destroy the Ork walker. Kyser's unit of guardsmen move to the north and open fire on the Weirdboy and his bodyguards, killing all of the boyz but unable to defeat the mocking psyker. In the center of the battle, the veteran troopers and the armored sentinel make a combined charge against the Ork Warlord and bodyguard, while the Bullgryns finally make their charge and are able to kill all of the Flash Gitz. The Scions with Orks on their heels shoot the grots holding the western objective and charge into what remains, kill all but the runtherd and capture the enemy outpost. The Orks reply in kind, and the reinforcement boyz storm a hostile objective and kill the scions in a charge that reclaims the west. On the southern front the trukk drives out of the melee and takes control over the southern objective. The Weirdboy leaves Kyser in the dust and teleports himself to the northern objective, attempting to destroy the Chimera with a psychic attack but is unable to damage the transport. The warboss and nob that destroyed the hellhound are able to finish the job with a few ranged shots that find the exposed weak points of the Chimera weakening it further, charge the armored sentinel and begin to damage it. The Ork Warlord leaves no prisoners and is able to finish off the sentinel with a crushing attack with his power klaw, but this leaves the veteran troopers open to kill off the remaining bodyguard.

- ![](09_hegesthes_border_raid/04-01.jpg "Kyser fells every other Ork moments before watching the psyker blink away")
- ![](09_hegesthes_border_raid/04-02.jpg "Control of the central objective hinges on a chaotic melee")

With their armored support gone the veteran troopers fall back from the Warbosses to make room for the tank. The Leman Russ turns its cannon to the Ork Warlord and with overwhelming firepower, blast the leader off the planet. The tank then performs a tank shock on the remaining warboss and kills the nob bodyguard. Captain Kyser and his troopers move double time in an attempt to retake the north from that pesky Weirdboy but are not able to clear that objective. On the southern side the Bullgryns charge the trukk but are unable to defeat it, and the vehicle's size ensures they are not able to control the objective either. Having survived the attack the trukk falls back and makes for the Imperial deployment zone. The Weirdboy once again leaves Kyser grasping at air as he teleports to the south in a failed attempt to finish off the last sentinel. Fresh from killing off the scions the boyz and runtherd charge through the ruins and with overwhelming force put an end to the veterans and take the center from them, turning to swarm the Leman Russ.

- ![](09_hegesthes_border_raid/05-01.jpg "Survivors along the southern front jockey for an advantageous position")
- ![](09_hegesthes_border_raid/05-02.jpg "For the moment, the Orks hold the central field")

With the Weirdboy gone and leaving such a tempting target exposed, Kyser retakes the northern battlefield, claiming a strong defensive point with lines of sight on the remainder of the battle extending his battle lines. The Bullgryns, having secured the south, charge to the center and engage the boyz in melee but are unable to kill them all. The Leman Russ' massive treads are able to finish off the last warboss. With Kyser preoccupied, the Weirdboy is able to teleport behind enemy lines and deploy a teleport homer in the imperial deployment zone to the east. The trukk on the south shoots across the battlefield and finds the weak spot of the Chimera, finally destroying the transport. Back in the center the Leman Russ squashes the runtherd, finishing what the scions began, and the Ork boyz and Bullgryns exchange casualties in melee.

With a near fully intact battle tank and Kyser and his troopers bearing down on the center, as well as lacking any intimidating leadership telling him he had to stay, the Weirdboy decides that now is as good a time as any to zog off and disappears in a zap of green light. The battle is ended and Captain Kyser has successfully reclaimed this outpost from the Ork counteroffensive, though it was a close victory for certain. The battle continues, and the war is ever being fought on Hegesthes.
