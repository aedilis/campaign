## War on Hegesthes

Boss Urlakk spent a lot of time making little progress in the long stalemate on [Hegesthes](#/maps/hegesthes), mostly fighting other Orks in the leaderless free-for-all since [Blakkjaw's](#/characters/urkrod-blakkjaw) defeat. However, he was one of the first warbosses to get word of the [humie 317th's](#/factions/hegesthean-dusters) streak of successes reclaiming territory when a [lone weirdboy](#/characters/wortskab) appeared outside his camp with news of some proppa enemies in *'Furd Krumpanee'*. Eager for a worthy fight, Urlakk set out for their position, amassing more followers as squabbling greenskins put their banter aside and joined up. As he marched east, he was intercepted by a small force of Tempestus Scions in an early test of his growing Waaagh!, but he [krumped them good and proppa](#/events/green-tide-rises). Bolstered by this early success, Urlakk pressed forth to find the [git responsible](#/characters/captain-kyser) for recent Imperial victories against the lesser warbosses and test his mettle himself.
