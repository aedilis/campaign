The Omadikea System is a T'au-aligned system in the Caldraeus Cluster. It is the center of the newly-formed [Daybreak Protectorate](#/factions/daybreak-protectorate).

# Background

The Omadikea System was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence on any of its worlds.

# M41

The extreme Warp activity near the Maelstrom affected the Omadikea System particularly acutely, and for decades it was so isolated from the wider Imperium that it was almost completely cut off. On its own, even its ability to keep its Imperial Tithe was far from enough to make up for a lack of trade and increased raider activity in the churning Warpstorm.

&nbsp;

When the ill-fated Fourth Sphere of Expansion scattered the T'au fleet, the colony ship under Commander Daybreak emerged from the Warp within the bounds of the Omadikea System. The T'au were looking to establish a new enclave, and more than willing to share their supplies from ships laden for a long colonial undertaking. When they also marshalled their defenses against the raiders that had long plagued the system, it took comparatively little work for the Water Caste diplomats to convince the humans to join their new union.

# M42

After years of integration, the T'au lead their enclave from [Omadikea](#/maps/omadikea), and even settled their Kroot auxiliaries on the jungle world of [Alektor](#/maps/alektor) which bears a resemblance to their home world of Pech. They now look to replicate their success in Omadikea and expand to the adjacent [Kathikos System](#/maps/kathikos-system).
