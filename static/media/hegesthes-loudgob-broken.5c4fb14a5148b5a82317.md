[ Incident Report ]

Approaching the hive sector indicated by Logister Akela to contain the data centers, Fourth Platoon came in from the southwest. Two Scout Sentinels were close to the first data center when they reported xenos presence. A mob of Greenskins, gretchin, and a transport had already begun harvesting one of the data centers using a crude device, no doubt given to them by one of their “Mek” class orks. 

The xenos came forward with speed, the transport billowing smoke as it made its way to confront the Sentinel at the eastern data center. A xenos psyker made its presence know by teleporting itself and a horde of Greenskins directly on top of another data center. Despite the distance, high pitched cackling and arguing hinted that the gretchin across the field had found something important. 

Making use of the Orks' overzealous push, Lt. Raphe issued orders. The Sentinels would cover the data centers and begin downloading the requested information by Vox link. The troopers Raphe led would spread out and guard the rear -- the seen Orks were fewer this long range auspex had predicted -- and the nearby Ogryns trotted alongside them. Recognizing the immediate threat of the psyker, Raphe ordered all available units to attack the large group of Orks on the central data center. The Platoon’s Hellhound unleashed a torrent of promethium flames that charred much of the unit. Expert maneuvers and timing form the Tempestus Scions had the storm troopers pouring out of the transport, in formation, while a hail of fire came from its turret. The combination attack was devastating and the elite troops finished off the Greenskins, including the large warboss leading them. There was only the psyker left who began a profane rhythmic chant and motion. The Battle Tank churned eastward to support the Sentinel.

- ![](20_hegesthes_loudgob_broken/01.jpg "")
- ![](20_hegesthes_loudgob_broken/02.jpg "")

Excited by the initiation of combat, the Orks came full on. Pouring out of the transport, the Ork elites and their warboss leader sprinted directly past the Sentinel and made for the Battle Tank, surely seeing the larger vehicle as the larger prize. Hacking into treads and peeling away armor plates with Power Klaws they shredded the tank to an inch of use. The transport rams into the Sentinel but neither do much damage to each other. A crackle and boom sounded as seven bolts of lighting solidified into a unit of meganobs and their warboss, though the teleportation must have disoriented them as they weren’t able to make a charge just yet. Finally the psyker finished its dance and disappeared in a green cloud.

- ![](20_hegesthes_loudgob_broken/03.jpg "")
- ![](20_hegesthes_loudgob_broken/04.jpg "")
- ![](20_hegesthes_loudgob_broken/05.jpg "")

Taking stock of the new threats, Lt. Raphe calls for action. He and his troopers move to place disciplined fire into the newly arrived meganobs, the Ogryns unleash a storm of bullets, and the Hellhound turns to open fire as well, collectively killing half of the unit. The Battle Tank attempts to get space but the brutal orks finally breach the hull as it pulls away, destroying it. Now exposed Bullgryns hurl their grenades into the unit of nobz and the scions combine with their taurox to shoot down the last nobs. The Bullgryns then rush forward and their clubs fell the warboss, leaving the East only with the trukk and Sentinel facing off. 

Regaining their bearings, the meganobs make a brutal charge, which results in the killing of all Ogryns and the near destruction of the Hellhound, which crushes one nob under its treads.

Having extracted all data, the Sentinel to the north crushes the terminal to ensure the foe can not claim any intel, the Scions finish removing the profane Orkish datathieves from the center and the Taurox now has line of sight on the gretchin which have been stealing the Imperium’s intelligence for the battle, unleashing all guns as the minuscule xenos are killed down to one remaining. In an effort to save the flame tank Raphe orders his troopers to fix bayonets. They charge into combat with the mega armored orks, Raphe cuts through power lines to bring one to its knees, and ends the creature with a sword strike through its eye. However, the mega armored warboss is able to destroy the Hellhound while the combat surrounds him.

Seeing the grots die, the xenos psyker teleports across the battlefield, attempting to steal from the terminal Raphe’s men secured at the start of the battle. One guardsman is outright killed by the meganobs and three are wounded, to whom the units medic administers Adrenaline shots and Instafoam healing salve to get back in the fight.

The Taurox clears the northeastern terminal of grots and begins Vox relay of requested data. The second Sentinel extracts the last intel from its terminal and starts to demolish it. Having cleared the field the Bullgryns rush to destroy any xenos infrastructure. The last melee is joined by a Sentinel as the last major threat in the battle. 

The Ork weirdboy begins bashing the terminal apart to stop data collection, while Raphe slays another meganob with the aid of his troopers.

The southern terminal is secured through destruction and the Sentinel turns toward the weirdboy. Distracted by his attempts to bash the terminal apart, the Sentinel is able to get a clean shot and remove the psyker threat with a single lascannon beam.

No more casualties are taken as all data centers have been cleared or controlled, all threats removed or surrounded. The battle is a strong victory for the Imperium.
