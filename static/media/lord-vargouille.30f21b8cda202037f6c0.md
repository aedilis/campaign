## Invasion of Hegesthes System

Entrusted with command of the Doom Fleet's troop deployment on Eryx after [his predecessor fell](#/events/knights-vs-chaos) to the Knights holding the Imperial line, Vargouille directed his troops to press into Ork territory, aiming to establish a wider base of operations to aid in the longer war against the well-entrenched Imperium. The Ork front is currently at a standstill, as both sides struggle to secure the first major victory along this new front.
