Gandymere is an Imperial agri world in the [Hegesthes System](#/maps/hegesthes-system).

# Background

Gandymere was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence here.

# M42

In 016.M42, after a group of [Ork freebooters](#/factions/bilge-squigz) were defeated by [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox), surviving Xenos landed or crashed on planets throughout the Hegesthes System, including Tengara. Defenders immediately engaged the threat, and so far, have been able to keep it largely contained.

&nbsp;

Given Gandymere's lack of major population centers as an agri world, the Orks invading initially found themselves bored, required to traverse great distances through crops and processors before finding a comparatively small fight. However, after realizing that the local human military traversed these great distances aerially, they threw themselves enthusiatically into the production of scrappy warplanes, and the battles for Gandymere took place largely in the skies.
