*The Last Breath* is the Emperor Class flagship of the [Doom Fleet](#/factions/the-doom-fleet), commanded by [Admiral Zaldrotha Voidrender](#/characters/zaldrotha-voidrender).

# The Doom Fleet Invasion

*The Last Breath* led the [Doom Fleet ambush](#/events/doom-fleet-invades) of 022.M42 into the Imperial [Belgatha System](#/maps/belgatha-system). *The Last Breath* personally destroyed two Firestorm Frigates and a Lunar Class Cruiser while the Doom Fleet destroys the rest of the [Fleet of the Amber Cloud](#/factions/fleet-of-the-amber-cloud), save the [*Aquila Illustris*](#/characters/aquila-illustris). With the battle won, the Night Lords controlled the space of the Belgatha System and began their campaign to conquer its planets.
