Belgatha is a formerly Imperial-aligned planet in the [Belgatha System](#/maps/belgatha-system). As the largest planet in the system, it exerts the most geopolitical influence on its neighbors, though it is ultimately accountable to the Ecclesiarchy-aligned government of [Caldraeus](#/maps/caldraeus).

# Background

Belgatha was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence here.

# M42

In 022.M42, after a group of [Night Lords](#/factions/the-doom-fleet) invaded and [defeated the Imperial fleet](#/events/doom-fleet-invades), their leader [Zaldrotha Voidrender](#/characters/zaldrotha-voidrender) began his conquest of the system. For Belgatha, this conquest was immediate, as the momentum from the invading ships' victory propelled them toward the most strategic planet in the system first, and the unprepared defenders were no match for the Heretic Astartes.

&nbsp;

Belgatha remains under Night Lords control, though with their limited numbers there is little they can do to take out all elements of Imperial resistance. Loyalists remain on the planet, seeking a way to undermine the enemy and return to the Emperor's light.
