The Ialaranth System is an Aeldari-controlled system in the Caldraeus Cluster. It consists of a single planet, the maiden world of [Ialaranth](#/maps/ialaranth).

# Background

Any attempt to approach Ialaranth from space is interrupted by a seemingly prescient counterattack from [Aeldari craftworlders](#/factions/viridian-weavers) materializing out of the Webway. As such, the space is largely avoided.
