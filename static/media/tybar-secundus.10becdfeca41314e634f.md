Tybar Secundus, often called just Tybar locally and known as Squig Run to the Orks, is an Ork-held planet in the [Tybar System](#/maps/tybar-system).

# Background

The Tybar System appears to have been within the domain of an ancient Necron dynasty centered on [Kalymnia](#/maps/kalymnia). Little is known about what became of this dynasty, and whether Tybar Secundus would have been a significant location to them.

# M41

In the 700s M41, as the nearby Hybraxis Nebula of the Maelstrom Zone faced the threat of a large Ork Waaagh!, xenos pillagers spilled into the Caldraeus Cluster as well. Though the Orks were eventually defeated, they had thoroughly overrun the Tybar, [Serres](#/maps/serres-system), and [Kalymnia](#/maps/kalymnia-system) Systems, and the Imperium strategically withdrew from all three.

# M42

The Ork dominion of the planet was interrupted when the [*Void Phantom*](#/maps/void-phantom) entered the system, and [a series of events](#/events/escape-the-mournful-sepulchre) caused the [*Mournful Sepulchre* Starfort](#/maps/mournful-sepulchre) to detach above the greenskins of Squig Run. Debris and ancient relics rained down on the planet surface, and the Orks briefly put aside their infighting to search and loot the exciting new drops, as well as fight some of the strange surviving creatures.
