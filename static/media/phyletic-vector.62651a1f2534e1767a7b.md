The [Plaguefester Warband](#/factions/plaguefester-warband) specializes in spreading contagion and causing widespread infection in the name of Nurgle. Key to their success has been the efficiency of the Phyletic Vector. Led by [Myasum Clostrid](#/characters/myasum-clostrid), a Plague Marine that was quickly noted for his ability to evolve to new environments to find the best way to spread the Grandfather's Gifts, the team has been instrumental in the Warband's endeavors.

# Background

The Phyletic Vector was among the main teams working on the [*Void Phantom*](#/maps/void-phantom) to turn it into a plague engine, but when it was invaded they shifted their focus to hunting down the invaders and making ready for the incoming Plaguefester Warband.

# Tybar

Having successfully escaped the wreckage of the [*Mournful Sepulchre*](#/maps/mournful-sepulchre), Myasum Clostrid and his Phyletic Vector now begin the process of preparing Tybar for the Plaguefester Warband's full arrival.
