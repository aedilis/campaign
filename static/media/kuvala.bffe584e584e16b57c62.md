Kuvala is an Imperial agri world in the [Caldraeus System](#/maps/caldraeus-system). It is explicitly governed by the Ecclesiarchy, though the Administratum and Militarum retain their traditional roles.

# Background

Kuvala was likely first settled by humans during the Age of Technology, who may have still been present in M31 during the Great Crusade when the Emperor declared the region *Pergatus* and unfit for further colonization efforts. Before M39, it was brought into Imperial control along with most Imperial settlements in the Maelstrom Zone.

&nbsp;

Any previous Xenos presence on Kuvala is unknown.
