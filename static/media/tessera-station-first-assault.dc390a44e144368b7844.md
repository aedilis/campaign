Tessera Station, a stronghold of strategic importance in this battle sector of [the Eryx war](#/campaigns/eryx-crusade). The raiding of the [Orks](#/factions/bilge-squigz) combined with the void assault and landing of the [Doom Fleet](#/factions/the-doom-fleet) have resulted in Imperial forces being stretched thin, supply trains are raided and commands are lost to the ghostly screams in the vox. Tessera Station stands as a bastion, communicae relay, and supply depot all in one vital complex, all eyes turn to it and seek its control.

It is in the outskirts of the station that [Castellan Rhys](#/characters/castellan-rhys), commander of the [Llyrian Drakes](#/factions/llyrian-drakes), fortifies his position as scouts report of an Ork warband gunning for their location. Rhys and [Commissar Schoenfeld](#/characters/commissar-schoenfeld) arrange their small recon element in the defensible center of the main approach to Tessera Station. As the Drakes dig in, a Krew of Freebootaz, [Da Badskwal Bukkaneerz](#/factions/badskwal-bukkaneerz), split to make a pincer attack against the defending Imperial Guard. The taktikz of [Kaptin Grogbreff](#/characters/gobsmakk-grogbreff) have the Guardsmen outflanked with [Morgrod Squigbita](#/characters/morgrod-squigbita) and [Gorgob Gitstompa](#/characters/gorgob-gitstompa) closing the gap to encircle the Llyrian defenders.

Rhys must defend and hold this location to ensure his foothold in the station while Grogbreff seeks to cement his position as WAAAGHboss by showing the other bosses how it's done and felling the strongest foe.

Castellan Rhys' recon troopers catch the Orks by surprise with their speed of redeployment, unlike the other forces of the Militarum they have fought previously. Seeing the outflank move, Rhys orders his troopers to take ground to form a defensive line, turning the encirclement into a pincer as the Steel Lance rough riders and trooper Squad Secundus rush through cover to take key points of control to the Southeast and Northwest. A team of lascannons move to take position in southern ruins as they train their sights for xenos vehicles to round the corner. The first shots of the conflict are made as the taurox prime forms the spearhead rushing the Southwest where Grogbreff and Squigbita are approaching. The rubble is too dense to fire at the Warbosses' trukks but they are able to eliminate the gretchin that were setting up a hold point. To the Northeast, Gitstompa's boyz were too eager to charge and their neglect of cover allowed the *Dragon's Fire* Executioner battle tank to open fire killing half of Gitstompa's bodyguard.

- ![](16_tessera_station/02a-01.jpg "")

Battle now engaged, Kaptin Grogbreff bellows a mighty WAAAGH!!! that is echoed in thunderous response by all the Orks. Bullet storms blaze in the sky as the Greenskins unload all of their dakka at everything but the targets of their charges. The ground quakes as choking trukk engines roar while a tide of green hurtles from the vehicle beds. Grogbreff sees the Taurox and bellows his challenge to the largest enemy in sight, his mighty power klaw tears armor panels and engine parts to shreds ripping fuel lines, treads, and pistons alike. An enthusiastic nob follows up after Grogbreff earning an angry glare for going for the Kaptin's kill, his power klaw scrapes into the engine in a shower of sparks that hit the torn fuel lines. An eruption of flames and fragmenting engine parts blast into the kill-stealing nob, earning a raucous guffaw from Grogbreff as the explosion kills the nob, carrying on to scorch the back of a scion and shred the front of three of Squigbita's boyz. The boyz, fueled by the explosions, lay into the scions with choppa and... choppa, hacking the squad to a man. Squigbita himself snicks his power klaw in anticipation as he squares up to the humie Weirdboy, and a single lighting-quick strike dashes the Psyker far more easily than Squigbita was hoping for. On the Northeast side Gitstompa and his remaining boyz make a great charge into the battle tank, but are counter charged by the Bullgryns in the ruins. Gitstompa's eyes are on the prize and he focuses down the battle tank substantially but not enough to brag about, while the Bullgryns finish off the remaining Boyz with him.

Rhys, now controlling enough territory to divide the Ork forces in half, orders for the Drakes to go on the attack. The *Dragon's Fire* splits its shots, sending its plasma across the field into Grogbreff and his nobs, but the Kaptin and his Krew are hearty and come out unscathed. The multi meltas, lascannon, and heavy stubber all fire on the solo Gitstompa but being so close he is able to dodge most of the fire, taking a few wounds. The lascannons in the south open fire on the now exposed trukks detonating it with the three super heated beams. [Alesis Telecarus](#/characters/alesis-telecarus), the Llyrian Drakes Enginseer, charges towards Gitstompa to protect the agent of the Omnissiah. In a duel between the two Telecarus' Omnissian Axe finds its mark as the enraged Tech-Priest defends the battle tank. Gitstompa falls beneath the Cog-blade of Mars. The Bullgryns, seeing that Telecarus has things under control, turn and charge warbikers that rumble their way out of the ruins, trading blows the two units each kill one of their enemies. In a heroic attempt at a flanking charge the Steel Lance lowers their melta lances and charges into the Flash Gitz, hoping to eliminate the ranged threat before they can draw line of sight. Underestimating their speed and prowess for combat the rough riders kill one Flash Git as the remaining four Cheap Shotz lower their snazzgunz to draw cruel looking choppas. Four of the rough riders are taken down as they learn that ranged Orks are still strong close combat fighters as well.

The Southwest of the battle now cleared, Grogbreff and his Krew jump back into the remaining trukk as it rumbles by, heading for the heart of the field. The outflank shows its true nature as a Deff Dread shambles its way into the battle to the Southeast on wildly hissing pistons. It levels its flame weapon at the lascannon team and despite their cover they are burned to one remaining. Squigbita, his trukk destroyed, exhorts his boyz into a charge that sweeps them into the Southern ruins to finish off the last lascannon, leaving the entire south of the battle in Ork control. To the Northeast, the trukk whips around firing its shoota at Alesis Telecarus but is unable to hit the Tech-Priest, and revs its engine as it prepares to charge the Bullgryns fighting the warbikers, together another Bullgryn falls beneath wheel and weapon. The last rough rider is able to strike true once more to slay another of the Cheap Shotz but the remaining three return the favor and turn to bring their snazzgunz to bear. 

Rhys and Schoenfeld still hold the line but are being increasingly boxed in by the Ork advance. Trooper Squad Secundus moves south from their strategic point and focuses fire on the trukk Grogbreff is riding in. The trukk survives as even plasma and melta shots are absorbed by the ramshackle transport. *Dragon's Fire*, now unengaged, reverses track and swings its weapons to aim at the newly arrived Deff Dread, a hail of superheated weapon fire slags the walker where it stands. Rhys, Schoenfeld, and trooper Squad Primus move through the ruins from the center to open fire on Da Cheap Shotz leaving only one alive.

- ![](16_tessera_station/05a-01.jpg "")

Grogbreff and Squigbita see the battle tank has come within striking distance and both charge to claim the prize of killing the biggest killer the enemy have. Massed attacks damage it heavily but are unable to destroy the tank thanks to Telecarus' field repairs. The last Cheap Shot tries to gun down Squad Primus but the dakka pings off the ruined cover and leaves the squad intact. The combat to the North continues as the last Bullgryn crushes another biker beneath his maul but is hurt by the twin attack. Grogbreff's second trukk, alone again, shoots at Squad Secundus unsuccessfully and guns its engine to run them over, but the uneven terrain sputters under the wheels stopping it from getting the speed to do any real damage.

The final Bullgryn pulls back out of the melee, and Squad Primus and the two Officers leave the last Flash Git to defend the center from the approaching Warbosses. *Dragon's Fire* falls back, allowing Rhys to give fire orders that result in Squigbita's boyz being killed except for the nob, and Grogbreff's Krew to be thinned out to one last nob. Seeing the Warbosses exposed, Telecarus' charges Grogbreff hoping to repeat his last kill, as Squad Primus charges Squigbita. The Orks roar with laughter as the Guardsmen fail to hurt anything more than their own pride. In response Grogbreff batters the Tech-Priest aside with ease, and Squigbita starts to thin out Squad Primus. In the Northwest Secundus and the trukk continue their fight.

Da Cheap Shotz' trukk charges the last Bullgryn but again is unable to hurt the abhuman. Grogbreff charges the battle tank and his last nob is killed in overwatch, and while he makes the charge the Leman Russ' armor is strong enough to avoid any damage. Castellan Rhys slays the last of Squibita's Boyz, as Squigbita, and his unbitten squig, take down the last of Primus. Melee rages in the center of the battle as Grogbreff hunts *Dragon's Fire* and Squigbita duels both Rhys and Schoenfeld.

- ![](16_tessera_station/07a-01.jpg "")

The end draws near as Squigbita strikes down Castellan Rhys with Schoenfeld unable to avenge him. Squigbita also turns and a sweep of his klaw catches the Commissar, sending him flying out of the fight. *Dragon's Fire* revs its engines, burning hot now, and leverages its massive weight to overcome Grogbreff, rushing over him in a churning mass of treads and dust.

- ![](16_tessera_station/08a-01.jpg "")

Despite enduring heavy losses and damage, the Llyrian Drakes blooded Da Badskwal Bukkaneerz for long enough that the remaing Flash Git and Squigbita mount up in their trukk and depart the battle leaving *Dragon's Fire* to be the sole holder of the line. Squad Secundus returns to its aid just after the Orks depart. Searching through the ruins Secundus finds the Officers, Castellan Rhys and Commissar Schoenfeld, wounded heavily but by the grace of the Emperor, just barely alive. Patching their leaders up enough to get them to the aid station the Llyrian Drakes successfully held the line and won victory this day.

*Morgrod Squigbita ducked behind the ruined pillars of the humie chapel as he considered his options. All in all, it had been a real proppa fight, and he had had the best time he could remember since he and Grogbreff crashed onto this world. He had gotten to skrag those shooty blue gits and their weirdboy, krumped the weedier humies with the big dakka guns, and duffed his way through more humies than he could count in the big tar pit that the center had become at the end. He was even pretty sure he got the two humie bosses personally, but couldn't be sure; he had certainly knocked them out of the fight, and they had stayed down, but he had to leg it when he saw the killy humie tank rolling toward him. He had just lost the last boy in his squad, and it was a lot less fun to face a tank that shooty when all of its guns would be trained on you before you got a chance to get into power klaw range.*

*Speaking of shooty, there was Sukkapunch, the leader of the Cheap Shotz flash gitz, rolling up to him in his trukk from the other side of the ruins.*

*"Dat tank looks pretty shooty, boss," the flash git offered, as if concurring with Morgrod's thoughts. "I fink it might be betta to come back fer anuvver go wiv a bit more dakka."*

*Morgrod considered this. He did hate to leave a fight, and that tank looked very skraggable, but he didn't want to be here if it rounded the corner and couldn't find any decent targets except himself. "Where's yer squad?" he asked Sukkapunch.*

*"Dedd," said Sukkapunch nonchalantly. "Got shot good and proppa by doze humies once dere bosses started yellin' at 'em ta shoot fasta. Da Kaptin's pretty bad too, I fink 'e lost a punchin' match wiv dat killy tank. I loaded 'im inta da back already."*

*Well that decided it, thought Morgrod. He wasn't going to be the last greenskin left for cleanup. Besides, this fight was* fun. *Imagine how much better it would be with more lads, and after the humies had a chance to reinforce better and bring some more of their mates.*

*"All right," he said, clamboring onto the back of the trukk with his semi-conscious kaptin. "Get us outta 'ere before dat tank spots us."*
