The Dark Paladin. The only force known to surpass his finesse of arms is his hatred of the foe. Reinhardt von Rosenherz has a storied history with a blade, and makes it his signature implement of death. Hailing from the noble house of Rosenherz on the Palatine Hive, it was not long before the recruiting eyes of the Adeptus Astartes noticed the fledgling boy. While he showed great promise with a sword, the zealotry and ferocity toward the impure that Reinhardt displayed was simply too much for the Imperial Fists to abide his induction in their own stoic chapter. Not wishing to waste his potential, however, he was passed on to the Imperial Fist's successor chapter: The Black Templars.

&nbsp;

Under Sergeant Kazimir, the Neophyte Reinhardt struggled for longer than many. No troubles were had with his intensive training, nor his skill with a blade, but rather his tendency to abandon his squad formations and expose himself delayed Reinhardt's acquisition of the Black Carapace. It was only through hundreds of hours instruction under Chaplain Siegfried that Reinhardt learned to control his rage and channel it as he might his blade. Now a powerful physical and spiritual force, Reinhardt found no trouble proving his admission into Marshal Eisenberg's Sword Brethren. When Eisenberg left to participate in the wars of the Pariah Nexus, Reinhardt was granted the rank of Marshal and dominion over the Valinor Crusade. This lasted until Eisenberg returned following the Vengeance Crusade, and took Reinhardt aboard the *Aegis of Iron*. While there, his rage caused the Battle Barge to redirect its course after a fleeing vanguard of Tyranids, the fallout of which caused their crash course into the Caldraeus Cluster.
