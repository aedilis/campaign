A freebooter cut from the cloth of his Blood Axe clan of origin, Kaptin Ghazbag Skraptoof combines the dedd Morky kunnin of a Blood Axe strategist with the dedd Gorky brutality of a renowned warboss and the dedd flashy style of a freebootin' pirate kaptin.

# Invasion of Caldraeus

Kaptin Skraptoof participated in the [Bilge Squigz](#/factions/bilge-squigz) invasion under [Urkrod Blakkjaw](#/characters/urkrod-blakkjaw) in 016.M42. After their naval defeat, his ship first landed on [Gandymere](#/maps/gandymere), where he and his ground forces were stymied by the agri-world's far-spread settlements. Skraptoof spent the ensuing period constructing a vast fleet of skrappy warplanes, favoring swarms of dakkajets to achieve overwhelming air superiority. This strategy rarely led to great success given the inexperience of his [enthusiastic pilots](#/factions/squigbuzza-skallywagz) and heroism of the planet's [well-trained defenders](#/characters/decker-bask). Frustrated by the lack of progress and opportunities for proppa combat, Skraptoof focused his efforts on a different sort of construction, and soon became the first Bilge Squig to successfully rebuild his destroyed ship and return to space, bent on visiting planets with a livelier scene.
