*Lothair could tell that there was fear.*

*Fear bled through the skin of mortals like sweat.*

*He could smell it. He could taste it. It gave him pleasure.*

*The darkness enhanced this sensation, the obscurity invigorated his blood.*

*Barely containing his energy, Lothair slipped on his helmet. Red cogitators displayed information and analysis of his surroundings but all of this was too much for his current hunger.*

*He only needed one scanner, and his wet voice spat out the raspy command to his armor's machine spirit.*

*"PREYSIGHT"*

#### +++EXCERPT FROM ENGINSEER TELECARUS' POST-BATTLE INTERVIEW WITH THE SURVIVORS OF TESSERA STATION+++
#### +++GUNNARY CORPORAL OF THE BLESSED MACHINE SPIRIT "DRAGON'S FIRE"+++
###

"She's a grand ol' machine, I'll tell ye that. *Dragon's Fire* been scorching Heretics and enforcing the Emperor's compliance across the system for centuries by my reckon. We keep her happy, and she's kept us safe. That's why we in the Cav group didn't get hit by the Scream. First volley must've hit the vox channels not but an hour after the Greenskins kicked dust out of this Hab-block. Earsplitting madness that stopped any chance of rest or focus. Throne above, even the sight of it... you could see all the Reggies buckling, holding their ears like their skulls were caved in. Barely let us get our bearings before the miasma came and flooded the field... but it was only when one of the PDF Reggies voxed in that they spotted some Astartes through the fog to reinforce us. That's when vets like us knew something was very, VERY wrong.

- ![](16_tessera_station/01b-01.jpg "")

"Sure didn't waste no time lighting the Damnable Traitors up with the main cannon. Ferrocrete blasted upon impact but it was the high pitch thuds of Ceramite that told our Auspex we took down Astartes with that volley. I was shocked as any when we broke through the fog that it was Terminator armor lying in the rubble. Round the corner some diabolical armor wheeled into range and threw some lascannon fire smack into our chassis only to ricochet right off. When the Scions voxed over that only a scattering of Riders got taken out, Sarge and I sort of broke into a nervous laugh. Surely the mythical sons of the Arch-Traitor weren't so fearsome as their legends?

- ![](16_tessera_station/02b-01.jpg "")

"So we pressed the attack. Our Auspex was struggling to train our weapons on targets within the miasma, so I popped my head out of the top port just in time to see our Reggies line up on no man's land. True to their cursed ego, the Traitors exposed their infernal tank directly to our twelve, so we let them have it. *Dragon's Fire* thrummed with hatred and engulfed the Predator in holy plasma. The heat must have detonated a fuel tank because the next thing we saw was a red hot marine writhing behind the crater we just made.

- ![](16_tessera_station/03b-01.jpg "")

"'For the Emperor!', Sarge was always the encouraging type, but even his cheers of praise couldn't keep my smile from dying on my face as I looked to the east: our left flank was struggling. Something unholy was happening over there. The fog was thick, but I could make out the ghastly light from a dozen or so sources. Something occult had bolstered those damn turncoats, and even the Emperor's finest soldiers couldn't chip away at the heretical rabble. Shadows of mounted guard, and outlines of brawny Bullgryns waded through streams of blue fire. They seemed slowed, like trying to run in a nightmare, and some diabolical energy held them from reaching their quarry. The Primus Reggies threw themselves at an incredibly outnumbered squad of lightning armored marines, the retribution that I witnessed sent me back into the *Dragon's Fire* and closed the hatch as swiftly as the Emperor allowed me.

- ![](16_tessera_station/04b-01.jpg "")
- ![](16_tessera_station/04b-02.jpg "")
- ![](16_tessera_station/04b-03.jpg "")

"The Vox was going mad. Interposed with the cries of maimed animals, grinding stones, dissonant chords, and tortured souls, the very real voices of my comrades from other squads coruscated through my skull, amplified by the hard Pla-steel walls of the *Dragon's Fire*. The ghostly Daemon Engine was vomiting plasma on our Riders, marines and terminators tore at Primus like independent articulated teeth of some fell beast. Every death rattle marked another line of defense broken between us and the evil force causing this gnashing pain. By the time I looked out our ports once more, our backline Heavy Weapons lay flayed across their guns with no enemies in sight, like they were shredded by phantoms. Schoenfeld's vox cut out, and by the Throne: Lieutenant Rhys was holding off the cursed maw by himself.

- ![](16_tessera_station/05b-01.jpg "")
- ![](16_tessera_station/05b-02.jpg "")

"Then, I saw the Warlord. The accounts of the burning blue eyes of the cultists was one thing, but there is a particular horror to look upon the visage of the arch enemy. These Astartes look like death, the old tales from Terra of blood hungry cretins whose skin is as pale as a corpse are nothing compared to the uncanny inhumanity of their eyes. Black holes, a void. Eyes are the windows to the soul and these beasts have no soul. The Warlord raised his accursed weapon, a sable mace rippling in crackles of purple energy. Summoning some evil sigil with his right hand, his followers died screaming from some unseen power. This illuminated the Warlord in occult fire, and I had to turn away as I saw his silhouette eclipse that of Lieutenant Rhys.

- ![](16_tessera_station/06b-01.jpg "")

"Turns out my retreat was well timed, as that ghastly plasma splashed across our side sponson. Our Corpsman checked on Sarge's burn marks as he inspected the hull for breaches. Bless that engine, she held. She's like us, and we don't break. The Scion's vox went cold, but they died before they broke. The Bullgryn's joyous call to "Protect the Little 'uns!" died out, but he didn't break. The Traitors had their chance to defend the Imperium, but now is our hour to be the Emperor's Hammer!

- ![](16_tessera_station/07b-01.jpg "")
- ![](16_tessera_station/07b-02.jpg "")
- ![](16_tessera_station/07b-03.jpg "")

"By this point we weren't receiving any more signals from the eastern front, so we had to assume the worst. Gunny put one last round of our sponson's pot shots down range where the Daemon Engine was. Can't say for certain if we got it but I heard something hiss. Then my port hole started getting clogged... bodies, unarmored, barely armed, and hardly clothed were seeping out of the ruined hab-blocks at the center of the battlefield. By all the damnable tricks these abominable Traitors can wield their capacity to corrupt the weak is among the most insidious. Sarge punched on the Promethium and *Dragon's Fire* roared toward the mob, but this must have been what the Scions had reported because through some uncanny chant these creatures so far from the light of the Emperor gained some Empyrean invulnerability. Their blood kept splattering against my port but Lord Commander strike me down if I am lying, they just kept getting back up. We weathered one more charge from the marines, this time close enough for me to see the skulls and skins of PDF stationed on the outskirts of this urban center decorating their midnight armor. A skull with red wings blasphemed their ancient plate. And like the bolts of lightning that adorned their armor, in a flash they were gone.

- ![](16_tessera_station/08b-01.jpg "")

"Finally the Taurox on the eastern front voxed back in. They couldn't hold back the enemy but just like with us, the foe just disappeared. I got to work hooking the plow up to the *Dragon's Fire* to help move the rubble and bodies. That's when we uncovered Commissar Schoenfeld from the heap of Primus that lay atop him. Sputtering but otherwise well enough, he began the recovery effort. You've likely already read the report from the eastern front so you'll excuse me if I don't recount that particular horror. It was Lieutenant Rhys that will stay with me until the Emperor decides I have served my time, however. They didn't kill him, no far worse. About an hour later scouts picked him up walking back toward Tessera station. Maybe it was his resolve, maybe it was shock, but his expression was that of stone. Not many men could keep their cool with the defiling they put him through. Barely a scratch on him, but the bones, flesh, and blood that hung off of him turned our stomachs, and the desecration of his beautiful golden Aquila shattered and sundered was the obvious Mark of Shame they left him with. Enginseer Telecarus offered him a replacement regalia, but the Lieutenant turned it away saying he "hadn't earned that honor yet." Damned fools they, if I know Rhys, their taunt will only be reason enough for him to fight all the more furious the next time those cowards try for us again. That was a damn hard fight, I just can't help but think that if those Traitors were only a little more ready for battle, would we have come away with such a draw? Either way we'll be ready. At least I will. My trigger finger has only gotten faster, and I'm pretty deadeye even without Auspex on the main cannon. For now, Tessera station is ours. The Emperor Protects, though I'm sure he appreciates our help."
