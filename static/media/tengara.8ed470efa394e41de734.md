Tengara is an Imperial death world in the [Hegesthes System](#/maps/hegesthes-system). With its relatively low population, it is mostly reliant on supplies and governance from [Hegesthes](#/maps/hegesthes) as well as other planets within the system.

# Background

Tengara was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence here.

# M42

In 016.M42, after a group of [Ork freebooters](#/factions/bilge-squigz) were defeated by [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox), surviving Xenos landed or crashed on planets throughout the Hegesthes System, including Tengara. Defenders immediately engaged the threat, and so far, have been able to keep it largely contained.
