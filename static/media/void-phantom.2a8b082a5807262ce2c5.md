The *Void Phantom* is an ancient Space Hulk that recently passed through the Caldraeus Cluster.

# Background

The *Void Phantom* spent millennia drifting through the galaxy. It eventually attracted the attention of the [Revenant Crusade](#/factions/revenant-crusade), when [Marshal Reinhardt](#/characters/reinhardt-von-rosenherz) steered the *Aegis of Iron* into it to fight the Archenemy aboard. The fused system of spacecraft entered the Warp, exiting again in the [Tybar System](#/maps/tybar-system) of the Caldraeus Cluster. This resulted in a separation of the [*Mournful Sepulchre*](#/maps/mournful-sepulchre), a Starfort that had long been fused into the aggregate Space Hulk, and bits of both the Hulk and the Fort rained onto the [planet below](#/maps/tybar-secundus). The sheer size of the *Void Phantom* meant this barely altered its trajectory, and it continues to slowly pass away from the system.
