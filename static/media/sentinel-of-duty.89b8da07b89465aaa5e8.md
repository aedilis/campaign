The *Sentinel of Duty* is a loyalist Imperial Knight from the feudal world of [Telemane](#/maps/telemane).

# Background

House del Rosin is one of Telemane's most reputable families, with a history closely tied to the Adeptus Mechanicus going back thousands of years. Boasting pilots of multiple Knights, they are well respected. Sir Gideon del Rosin, pilot of the Knight Paladin *Sentinel of Duty*, lives up to his House name in his defense alongside the Telemane Garrison.

# The Telemane War

The *Sentinel of Duty* was present at the [first invasion of Telemane](#/events/defense-of-telemane), where it led the Imperial defenders to the first victory the [Belgatha System](#/maps/belgatha-system) had experienced against the invading [Doom Fleet](#/factions/the-doom-fleet). Gideon del Rosin won accolades for his house by defeating the traitor knight [*Umbra Mortis*](#/characters/umbra-mortis) in a dramatic knightly duel.

&nbsp;

The *Sentinel of Duty* was not present at the [fall of Hive Aspidopolis](#/events/battle-of-hive-aspidopolis), and its fate is currently unknown. Many Imperial resources were evacuated to be redeployed on [Eryx](#/maps/eryx), and still more stayed behind to resist the occupiers; it is possible the scion of House del Rosin is among either group.
