The Belgatha System is a formerly Imperial-aligned system within the Caldraeus Cluster. As with all Imperial systems in the cluster, it fell under the jurisdiction of the Ecclesiarchy-aligned government of [Caldraeus](#/maps/caldraeus). Specific system control was determined by the government of its most populous planet [Belgatha](#/maps/belgatha), with a high degree of autonomy among its planets.

# Background

The Belgatha System was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence on any of its worlds.

&nbsp;

Worlds were defended by local PDF and potentially regiments from elsewhere in the cluster. Its strongest defensive asset came from the Knightly Houses on the planet [Telemane](#/maps/telemane).

# M42

In 022.M42, the system was invaded by the Night Lords [Doom Fleet](#/factions/the-doom-fleet) under [Zaldrotha Voidrender](#/characters/zaldrotha-voidrender). Despite the defense of [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox) and the [Fleet of the Amber Cloud](#/factions/fleet-of-the-amber-cloud), the loyalist fleet was [quickly defeated](#/events/doom-fleet-invades), and Zaldrotha consolidated control of the space around the system. He continued his lightning invasion with an immediate capture of Belgatha itself. Shortly after, he [sent a lieutenant](#/events/naxos-besieged) to conquer [Naxos](#/maps/naxos), and eventually won the war for Telemane by 024.M42, establishing control of the final planet in the system.

&nbsp;

The Night Lords currently control each planet, but with their small numbers, they will not be able to stamp out all Imperial resistance. Loyalists remain present on Belgatha and Telemane, operating underground and attempting to undermine the enemy wherever possible.
