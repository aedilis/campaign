<h3 className="ork-heading">In Da Beginnin'</h3>

<p className="paragraphs ork-speech">After the Orks took over the <a href="#/maps/tybar-system" className="site-link ork-link">Tybar System</a> in the 700s M41, they lost the unity they had in the conquering Waaagh! and looked for new fights amongst each other and their environment.</p>

<p className="paragraphs ork-speech end-of-section">Over the course of the years, the Orks gravitated into two major factions. Da Beast Krumpaz focused on hunting and taming the planet's massive beasts and squigs, while <a href="#/factions/bonebreakaz" className="site-link ork-link">Da Bonebreakaz</a> favored elite infantry and heavy armor.</p>
