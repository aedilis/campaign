The Plaguefester Warband hails from the bowels of the Warp, Nurgle's servant Astartes dedicated to the spreading of putrid plague and maladies.

&nbsp;

Currently led by the Lord of Contagion [Mophilus Prion](#/characters/mophilus-prion), the Plaguefester Warband latched onto a drifting [Space Hulk](#/maps/void-phantom) in the Warp and began to infect it, intent on using the hulk for nefarious deeds.

# Background

The Death Guard company began constructing a vile machine in the heart of the hulk that when activated would spread plague and sickness through all systems it passed through, like a massive, infected censer. The Hulk left the Warp and upon entering real space was detected by [nearby Astartes](#/factions/revenant-crusade) of the Black Templar Chapter, who came to investigate. Once the nature of the hulk was revealed to them, the Plaguefester Warband had to fight to continue their foul works. While far from defeated, the Death Guard were delayed in their construction long enough for the Space Hulk to slip back into the Warp where [even more fighting](#/events/space-hulk-crucible) happened in the confined corridors of the hulk.

&nbsp;

Once more the Plaguefester Warband enters [real space](#/maps/tybar-system) as the hulk bursts into the Caldraeus Cluster, hurling fragments to the various systems, and while the schemes around the Space Hulk have been foiled, Mophilus Prion and his Plaguefester Warband have found a new location that is ready to be ripened with the putrid sicknesses of Nurgle and the Death Guard.
