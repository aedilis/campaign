Da mekz told us ta gavver as much uv dat kind as we could, an' we told 'em dey ain't da boss, iz dey, so go take a swim in da dropz. Whole squadz uv boyz would disappear, not just da grotz, and we kept findin' shellz and dakka blast marks dat none uv da ladz claimed woz from dem skrappin'. 'E laid sum uv our best skrap out and started bellowin' "Come and get it ya creepy zoggin' beakiez!" Soon enuff, I saw dese hulkin' humiez in dedd 'ard armor just sorta appear out of da mist an' shadowz. Dunno 'ow dat worked for gitz as big as dey were. Didn't much care eivver, we finally 'ad a fight! WAAAGH!!!*

The Ork trukks revved their engines and cheered as the Night Lords made their appearance from the shadows between the Imperial ruins. Thick rubber wheels crunched as they sped over the precious loot they had laid out to advertise their location, its value forgotten to the bosses who had intended to snatch it back up before the enemy could take it. The Night Lords suffered no such lapse in judgement. The Warpsmith extracted the nearest cache embedded in a derelict station as the rest trained their guns on the advancing xenos vehicles. Concentrated fire from the *Heretic's Fork* in coordination with the Flensing Fangs tore the Ork boss-kaptin's trukk into rent metal just before the traitor guardsmen of the Night Watch opened fire on the figures staggering out of the wreckage.

*"Who da zog blew up me trukk?"* bellowed Grogbreff as he rolled out of the burning heap behind him. His eyes first aligned on the glint of ceramite from within a fortified bunker in the midfield. *Probably dem*, he decided, and launched himself over the broken roof, klaw swinging. One of his nobz landed just to his left, right as Grogbreff realized they were lucky to fit even two Orks into the crammed pillbox along with five angry frames of the power-armored spikey beakiez. Fortunately, the obvious solution was to subtract beakiez, and that happened to be Grogbreff's plan in the first place. His bellow of rage turned into one of exuberance as he slashed and snapped with his upgraded klaw, firing his shoota into the air more for the atmosphere than any realization he might direct the dakka at a target. His nob swung just as wildly with him, and based on the number of nobz piling in on top of fallen bodies, Grogbreff assumed they must be making progress. He had been having much too good a time to count the enemies they were taking out, even if he could have counted that high in the first place.

The sergeant of the Flensing Fangs seemed to practically stitch together out of nothing just outside the collapsing pillbox he had been in moments ago, cloaked in darkness. Judging from the continuous shouts and shots within the bunker behind him, the simple-minded xenos didn't even realize that he was gone, or that his four brothers had already fallen. Just as well; no pursuers meant the precious data in the undefended center of the battlefield was his to seize, and the pathway to the heart of the corpse-worshippers' defenses would be his lord's to unlock. He sneered at the thought of how these aliens paled in comparison to their real goal. True enough, it had been pleasant sport harvesting them in lightning raids as the warband had gathered intel and realized the opportunity their stolen technology offered to infiltrate the Eryxian defenses, but the Orks were no prey at all compared to the Imperium. They weren't even strategic enough to manage a worthy challenge in battle; there was no enemy unit between him and his prize, save a single clamoring vehicle whose guns couldn't penetrate his armor on their best day. And that ridiculous vehicle wouldn't even be able to harry him, as he watched their Predator Annihilator train its guns on the smoking transport and find its mark.

The fireball from the exploding trukk blasted him off his feet, and he once again knew darkness.

Squigbita spat a tooth out of his bloody mouth as he straightened up from his trukk's explosion, then realized it wasn't even his and quickly pocketed it before the dazed owner noticed. The mess of bodies littering the wake of his trukk's deadly demise stirred as the survivors picked themselves up, just as the warboss noticed the line of sight it had left open between them and some nasty-looking humies dug into a ruin some ways away. He heard the sharp report of a single bullet and felt a sting in his center of mass; one of those humies was a good shot. A blow like that could have felled a weaker git, and Squigbita didn't want to wait around to find out how many more they had. He hugged the walls of a ruined pillbox across from Grogbreff's and waited for his surviving boyz to join him.

Squigbita glanced over to see how his kaptin was doing. The answer seemed to be *not great.* Grogbreff was apoplectic, roaring something about a stolen kill as he gesticulated wildly at the body of a spikey beakie impaled by a rebar from Squigbita's ruined trukk. His nobz seemed pretty surly too, at least until they all remembered there was a skrap going on when a series zappy green blasts, like you sometimes get when a weirdboy gets too weird, filled their bunker. The stink of cooked Ork meat reached even Squigbita's nostrils across the way, but it seemed like it snapped Grogbreff out of his jealousy, as the kaptin and a single surviving nob scattered out of the enclosed splash zone they had unwittingly left themselves in.

Squigbita craned his neck around his own protective wall to trace the source of that dakka. And by Gork, did he see it. Some massive, gribbly squig in forbidding armor lumbered about the battlefield, leveling a gun so big Squigbita couldn't believe a creature could carry it without a trukk or wagon. The gribbly squig towered above even the massive tank that had blasted Squigbita's ride out from under him, and immediately updated Squigbita's priorities for what he wanted to fight next.

Lord Reshig Vargouille pounced lightly onto the cratered ground, the five terminator bodyguard of his Tremorhost joining him with a grace that belied their massive size. Something was wrong. All across his warband, though his units gathered the strength they needed from their dark pacts with the gods, the gods were extracting an unusually steep price. The traitor guardsmen had members disappear from their ranks before the very eyes of those who remained, and his Forgefiend seized and shuddered even as it laid down fire into the highest priority targets. Even his Warp Talons, who materialized from the warp to eviscerate a squad of Ork bikers attempting to outflank the Warpsmith, found themselves unable to jump fluidly after a single Ork biker escaped their assault badly wounded. At least the strangely reliable cultists of the True Vessels made short work of the survivor, but still, it was almost as if the gods were displeased with them. And now another complication, as he realized he had lighted upon the battlefield just out of range for a clean kill on the Ork warboss and warriors holding this corner of the battlefield by themselves. He needed the fractured Imperial artifact buried within their crude barricades to continue his real work against the Imperium, but now the brutish boss there would get it before he could.

Vargouille peered closer. That warboss looked familiar.

As he cleared the collapsed wall of the bunker, Kaptin Grogbreff saw his deff dread walker finally appear for the flank he had ordered at the start of the battle. Two enormous spikey beaky war machines appeared tantalizingly close, and between Grogbreff, Squigbita's ladz, and the deff dread, the kaptin could practically taste that it was time for a flashy kill. He bellowed a mighty *WAAAGH!!!* and heard it taken up by Orks across the scattered battlefield. The massive gribbly war engine swung its guns toward him, but he had just weathered its last round of dakka; Grogbreff knew it couldn't reload in time to catch him before he closed the gap. *Except for da fact dat gribbliez are cheaty gitz*, a voice faintly echoed in his head, a quarter second before more blasty green dakka filled his field of vision.

Squigbita had been stuffing his pouches full of promising-looking loot he found strewn about the center of the battlefield when he heard Grogbreff's warcry. He saw his kaptin's last nob fry in a blast of weird warpy dakka, and Grogbreff himself paused, stunned and blinded, in the middle of the field. The deff dread's piston legs shot up and down impressively, but not exactly forward, leaving Squigbita as the one element of what had moments ago looked like a three-pronged charge.

Support or no support, it was time to get stuck in. Squigbita echoed Grogbreff's warcry and rounded the corner of his bunker with a roar, his ladz in tow. The giant gribbly squig's guns were still trained on Grogbreff, and Squigbita grinned as he felt his klaw make contact with the twisted, profane metal of its ghastly armor. Klaw blows and choppa blows rained in and out, and for all its size, the twisted engine could not withstand the ferocious onslaught of Orks fueled by their leader's call to war.

With a resounding crack and horrific roil, the monstrosity buckled, ruptured, and burst into pieces around him. A horrible scrap of pallid flesh landed at his feet with a wet plop. Squigbita scooped it up and tore a piece off with his teeth. He chewed thoughtfully, frowning. "Dis ain't a squig at all," he muttered to nobody in particular.

Vargouille heard a great warcry originate from the center of the battlefield, then heard it taken up by the warboss charging him. *Gitstompa*, he remembered with disdain. The leader he had fought to a stalemate on his first attempt to break through to the human defenders of Eryx was apparently still alive. But even with the momentum of his lord's crude motivation, this beast was no match for Vargouille's subterfuge. The entire Tremorhost were one with the shadows, disappearing before the Orks could swing their primitive weapons, reappearing behind them just in time to snuff them out, repeating it all for the next alien down. The brutal charge would have flattened his terminators if uninterrupted, but a scant two Orks remained at their master's side by the time the xenos blades could finally meet their original targets.

At last, the Night Lords would be delivered their deserved upper hand. Vargouille watched with satisfaction as his Warp Talons found their mobility once again and descended upon the diminutive creatures the Orks had left unattended to guard a valuable outpost behind their lines. Around him, his guard staggered under the furious Orkish counterattack, until the last wounded terminator was flattened by their ramshackle transport's wrecking ball, but it was enough. Vargouille cut down the last Ork warrior accompanying the warboss, satisfied he had robbed them of their critical advantage against him, and that he could now make short work of the general that had bedeviled him from the start.

With the big gribbly squig-like engine destroyed, and the smaller humies accompanying the spikey beakiez mopped up in short order, Grogbreff and Squigbita found themselves with few boyz and one very large spikey dakka-tank. The tank in question had skidded up the battlefield, leaving the shambling deff dread snapping uselessly at its dust trails, and let loose with its weaponry. Just two of the Flash Gitz that had accompanied Grogbreff from the start in his doomed trukk remained standing after its small arms fire went quiet, but the wounded Grogbreff himself was the target of its strongest blasts. Though the gunner's aim was true, Grogbreff leered in satisfaction as he felt his lucky blue coat (possibly aided by his expensive armor) dissipate the energy harmlessly. If these gitz thought he would go down like some yoof, it was time to give them a proppa edukashun.

Grogbreff raced to the vehicle's right flank just as Squigbita and his few surviving ladz closed in on its rear. Grogbreff sized up the smoke coming out of the tank and decided one good swing ought to do it. "ME FURST! ME FURST! DAT'S AN ORDER!" he roared, rending deep into the vehicle's side armor as his power klaw crackled. It was a beautiful hit, as deep as he could have wanted. Hmm, actually. Maybe a bit too deep even.

Squigbita's klaw ripped into the vehicle's back plating, along with his attendant nob's klaw and a few flashing choppas. Vargouille and Gitstompa swung powered blow against massive riposte, again and again, as each stood amidst a mound of bodies of his own former guard. Murderous hatred filled the Night Lord's eyes, while blood and spittle flecked the Ork's roaring lips. Vargouille sliced the Ork deeply, but winced as its klaw closed around his side, coming dangerously close to breaking through the ceramite. For an impossibly long time, the stalemate continued. Vargouille could see that he was more bloodied than the Ork boss, and that sooner or later, the wildly swinging wrecking ball of the creature's irritating transport would make a lucky connection. He realized this might be closer to the end than he planned.

As if the gods caught his flicker of doubt, Vargouille began to feel a subliminal tug. Looking down, he saw his left leg shrouded in trickling, liquidy shadow, that passed over his armor and moved on to reveal emptiness where his limb had once stood. The anomaly spread across his lower body, winking him out of existence, punishing him by removing him from battle. Vargouille could sense himself reappearing safely, shamefully, at his operating base.

"Oh no ya don't!" Gitstompa barked. "Yer not gettin' away again!" "ZOGGIN' *FANK* YOU!" Grogbreff told the spikey mek, before ripping him in two with his klaw.
