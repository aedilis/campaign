# Background

Greenskins have held the Tybar System since their war with the Imperium a generation ago; now, the Orks that remain squabble amongst each other in small bands. One such band is led by the nob Grazbog; his handful of boyz never grew enough to function as a true army, but without an organized foe or proper warboss, they were content to target other small groups and poke around the ruins and old battlefields for good loot.

# Crash of the *Mournful Sepulchre*

When the Starfort first broke off its Space Hulk and crashed onto Squig Run, it began a frenzy of Ork warbands swarming to the crash sites to loot the fun new bits and fight any strange gribblies that had landed with them. As an added bonus, the sudden influx of competing Ork bands proved a rousing good time for each group to shoot and clobber the others to their hearts' contents. Most of the loot was strange and difficult to puzzle out a good Orky use for, even among some of the kunnin meks, but Grazbog was very pleased to find a box full of knobs and levers that emitted all kinds of horrible screeches and feedback when you fiddled with it.
