Admiral Umbranox is an Officer of the Fleet who currently holds command of the [*Aquila Illustris*](#/characters/aquila-illustris). He and his armada are the Emperor's first line of defense against any Maelstrom or xenos incursion into the Caldraeus sector, and he finds himself tasked with protecting the planets and outposts most vulnerable to the cluster's current crises.

# Background

Otto Geriatricus Umbranox was the son of a planetary governess and entered into the Schola Progenium early; with a knack for naval command, he quickly became an officer after leaving the Schola. Just over a century later, Umbranox commanded an Emperor-class battleship as head of the [Fleet of the Amber Cloud](#/factions/fleet-of-the-amber-cloud).

# Blakkjaw's Attack

In 016.M42, Ork freebooters known as the [Bilge Squigz](#/factions/bilge-squigz) invaded the Hegesthes System, but their armada was quickly broken up by the fleet under Umbranox. While survivors maneuvered their broken ships into a few nearby planets, Umbranox's fleet stopped what could have become a Waaagh! throughout the entire cluster, and ended the centralized threat of the Orks' original leader, [Kaptin Blakkjaw](#/characters/urkrod-blakkjaw).

# Doom Fleet Invasion

Caught out of position by the lightning invasion of [Belgatha](#/maps/belgatha) under [Zaldrotha Voidrender](#/characters/zaldrotha-voidrender) in 022.M42, Umbranox's entire fleet was [destroyed in moments](#/events/doom-fleet-invades), save the *Aquila Illustris* from which he held command. Umbranox responded as quickly as he could, dispatching bombers to destroy a damaged Murder Class Cruiser and escaping the ambush as soon as the successful pilots returned from eliminating the enemy. Umbranox saved the invaluable flagship with his tactical retreat, and raised the alarm throughout the cluster as he began attempting to marshal a response with the broader Caldraeus fleet.

&nbsp;

The loyalists of the [Naxos](#/maps/naxos) orbital stations were able to transport some of the damaged hulks of the Fleet of the Amber Cloud back for repairs, and were in the process of doing so when they came under attack by the Voidrender's agent [Lord Malithor](#/characters/lord-malithor). While Malithor eventually succeeded in [conquering the orbitals](#/events/naxos-besieged), the Imperial defenders bought enough time to deliver refugees and supplies back to Umbranox along with three ships made ready for combat once again: the *Purity of Dawn*, the *Pillar of Conquest*, and the *Star Hunter*. Together with Umbranox's *Aquila Illustris*, the reconstituted fleet made ready to defend, and perhaps begin reclaiming, Imperial space.

&nbsp;

The Fleet of the Amber Cloud was last seen back in the Belgatha System, as the crumbling world of [Telemane](#/maps/telemane) sent surviving military and knights off planet before they were lost to the Voidrender's [final push](#/events/battle-of-hive-aspidopolis). The evacuee ships rendezvoused with Umbranox's fleet, as they provided safe escort to where the resources would most need to hold the line: [Eryx](#/maps/eryx), the site of the Night Lords' latest theater of expansion.
