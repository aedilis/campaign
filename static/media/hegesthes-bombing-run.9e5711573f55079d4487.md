> QUOTATION
>
> Dem 'umie gits didn't even bring proppa fightas! We'll blast dem bommas outta da sky easy!
>
> &mdash; Flyboy Rotzod, moments before his dakkajet explodes in a hail of lascannon fire

The [317th Hegesthean Regiment](#/factions/hegesthean-dusters) has dispatched two bombers with a Thunderbolt escort to level the Ork encampment ahead of a ground assault. At the sound of incoming planes, a team of Ork flyboys hoot and cheer as they hop into their dakkajets and race up to meet them.

Watching the range close from long to medium, the Imperial planes unleash their firepower to protect the bombers. One dakkajet kitted out with extra shootas makes an easy first target, and one more is shot out of the sky along with it in the initial salvo. The Orks pour what shots they have into one of the bombers, but despite the dizzying amount of bullets fired, there is only so much they can do to the heavily armored hull.

![right-1.5](02_hegesthes_camp_raid/dakkajets_chase_bombers.jpg "Dakkajets chase the bombers as they release their payloads.")

The bombers level out their flight as low as they can go and drop their payloads, each obliterating the targets underneath them. Only one depot remains as the dakkajets chase after the mobile fortresses like frenzied squigs, pouring more dakka out but struggling to make a dent. Their ground gun finally destroys one heavily damaged Marauder Bomber, but the other has enough time to reposition itself and opens its bomb bay once more over the final Ork asset. With the mission accomplished, one more Thunderbolt falls to the Ork turret before all remaining Ork planes are shot down and the heavy cannon itself goes up in flames amidst strafing fire.

The Imperial Navy has done its job: the mission will now be [decided by the Emperor's foot soldiers below](#/events/hegesthes-camp-raid).
