*Ever since the [first fight with the bugeyez](#/events/the-splinter-fleet-arrives), Krogskull Gobrekka knew there had to be a way to get to wherever the creatures had come from and give them a proppa kickin', and he was determined to find it. A team of oddboyz led by his big mek Badratchit had been dutifully looting the crashed humie tech, occasionally producing some loud zaps and blips that resulted in new pockets of bugeyez appearing nearby. This was always greeted with a roar of approval from the assembled boyz, who got stuck in with gusto. The bugeyez liked to get in close, so it was always a proppa scrap, although a few of the gits always seemed to peel off and make a beeline for the trees to disappear while the Orks were distracted. But that was probably fine.*

*And thus the work had carried on, until Badratchit finally told the boss he had tinkered with the gubbins just right, and they were ready to tellyport from their end to wherever all the fun was and really get to scrappin'. First, Gobrekka and Badratchit rolled a bunch of squigs into the chamber and tellyported them off, but eventually realized the squigs wouldn’t be able to report back and so stuffed a bunch of protesting grots into the chamber to tellyport them instead. Then they realized they had no way to tellyport the grots back from their end anyway, so said zog it and loaded into the chamber themselves.*

*With a zap of bright blue humie lighting, along with some orky green sparking that Badratchit had added just to make the whole process a little more proppa, Gobrekka’s boarding party disappeared from Squig Run.*

*A short stretch of confusing color and jarring acceleration later, they found themselves in a dark, vast room, silent but for some low whirring, with narrow corridors jutting outward into Mork knows where. They were surrounded on all sides by ancient metal panels that matched the scrap that had fallen across Squig Run all of a sudden one day and kicked off a spree of looting and wrecking amongst the resident greenskins. Gobrekka’s tellyported squigs bounded about the metal floor of the chamber with bugeye bits in their mouths, while grots clung to the walls and dangled from the ceiling doing their best to avoid them. This must be the place, but what was it?*

*Badratchit headed straight for a series of consoles across the platform and flipped a massive lever he recognized from the wreckage on Squig Run. A faint whirring sound started up, but the mek had the disappointed look of someone who expected something more exciting. His eyes traced a series of wires from the console down one of the hallways.*

*"Should be more switches down dere, boss," he told Gobrekka, pointing helpfully. "If you can flip 'em, I can get dis bit ta work." That sounded dedd boring, but the warboss couldn't think of a faster way to find more bugeyez, so he grunted a reluctant affirmation and set out toward the bulkhead door. Hopefully he'd find something to krump while his mek mucked about.*

*He didn't have to go far.*

![separator](orks/orks.svg "")

Gobrekka smelled something far worse than anything he had encountered at the drops or the squig pens, and it was only getting stronger. As he entered an open hallway it grew almost enough to make his eyes water, and he finally saw the source. Hulking armored beakies, surrounded by such an evil-smelling aura that he wanted to wretch on the spot, advanced slowly toward him. They were green, but no proppa green; a sickly imitation of the good Orky green, pale and wrong. He hated them immediately.

They were ready for him, and fired putrid gauntlet projectiles as he entered the open walkway. Gobrekka roared his displeasure and charged. He slammed into them and heaved his huge choppa at the closest after rebounding off. It crashed through the armor and squelched wetly into whatever lay beneath, but despite the staggering blow, the thing kept its feet. *These gits can take a hit!* Gobrekka felt his foul mood start to shift. *Maybe this scrap wouldn't be so bad.*

Behind them, a bulkhead door burst off its hinges and Gobrekka's underboss and nobs came out swinging. The big boss-looking beakie didn't seem to anticipate this, and Gobrekka barked a laugh as he watched him take a power klaw to the gob before he could ready a parry. Armor shattered and something foul bubbled out through the cracks. The beakie boss stumbled back lifelessly and tore rusted grating apart as he plummeted down to the floors below.

A hail of gunfire snapped Gobrekka's attention from the fight down the corridor the horrible fake-green beakies had come down. A squad of smaller beakies had opened fire, and the underboss's nobs dropped like flies. Then it was Gobrekka's turn to be surprised by a door swinging opening, and another squad charged out behind him. The last of his meganob bodyguard fell to this new onslaught, but the newcomers would just have to wait their turn if they wanted Gobrekka's attention. He swung his choppa with renewed vigor at the heavily-armored beakies and felt one fall, but the last of them sloughed off what should have been a fatal wound as Gobrekka found himself alone and surrounded. He beamed.

The corridor was a mass of bodies living and dead as everyone still able to move piled in. His underboss and surviving nobs joined in on his left, his backup squad of meganobs caught up on his right, and Gobrekka hacked the last of the megabeakies to pieces. Half a dozen of the remaining beakies fell to his mates, but Gobrekka grimaced as the survivors focused their blows against him. These things packed a punch, and that awful miasma filled his throat and made him feel sluggish and weak.

*Time ta end dis*, Gobrekka thought to himself and readied his choppa. The beakies must have agreed, because almost in concert on either side of him a beakie raised a glowing blue pistol. Each pulled their trigger, and each exploded into searing balls of light. Gobrekka blinked in confusion in the afterglow. *Did dey mean ta do dat?* You could never tell with humies and their dodgy tek. Give him reliable Ork engineering every time.

The final two beakies didn't last long, and Gobrekka let himself exult in the post-combat thrill and slightly cleared air for a moment before remembering he had a job to do here.

He ground his teeth in frustration; this gift from the gods was supposed to happen in the battle, not after. He lifted his weapon and walked slowly back toward his base. It would seem that more work awaited him than he had anticipated.*

*"All right Badratchit, we flipped all da shiny bits," Krogskull growled impatiently through the comms device his mek had fashioned for him. "Now what?"*

*"Jus' a tick, boss!" Badratchit chirped from the command center by the tellyporta chamber. He hummed contentedly as he flipped levers and pressed buttons along the ancient humie consoles in ways that he thought probably made sense. The grots he had impressed into service scuttled around welding wires together and ripping panels open, dodging swinging tools and the bounding squigs that were getting bored of the bugeyez scraps they had been tugging out of each other's jaws. Power pulsed through the console for the first time in untold years and crackled out of loose wires and rent connections.*

*Badratchit wasn't exactly sure what he was doing, but his instincts had never failed him. Well, that wasn't true, but they were usually trustworthy. Well, that wasn't true either, but he had a good feeling about this time. He calibrated dials and gears until he felt satisfied, then punched a large central button. Lights flicked on to full brightness in his chamber, and clicks echoed down the hallway as power spun out and coursed through the ship's arteries.*

*The mek started as a rusty speaker in a fragile humie skull that he had imagined was for decoration crackled to life next to him.*

*"LIGHTHOUSE PROTOCOL IN EFFECT. EXTREME WARP OBSTRUCTION DETECTED. BROADCASTING CURRENT LOCATION ON ALL FREQUENCIES AT MAXIMUM STRENGTH. WARNING: UNENCRYPTED COMMS. UNFRIENDLY OPERATIVES CAN INTERCEPT. TO OVERRIDE--"*

*Badratchit shattered the ancient bone to dust with the heavy end of his spanner. "I don't speak humie," he informed the rubble, and resumed tinkering.*
