The *Mournful Sepulchre* is an ancient Starfort that has recently appeared within the Caldraeus Cluster.

# Background

The *Mournful Sepulchre* spent millennia attached to the aggregate Space Hulk designated the [*Void Phantom*](#/maps/void-phantom). It drifted along with the Hulk through the galaxy, until eventually it entered the [Tybar System](#/maps/tybar-system), when it was dislodged from the Hulk amidst [fighting between Black Templars and Death Guard](#/events/escape-the-mournful-sepulchre). Several structures and chambers crashed onto the [planet below](#/maps/tybar-secundus), but the Starfort itself remained mostly whole, joining the planet in orbit as part of the system while the *Void Phantom* continued drifting away.

# M42

Eventually, Orks on the planet below appear to have worked out a way to reverse the connection on a crashed teleportarium chamber, and a few have used this to enter the Starfort directly from the surface of the planet. It remains to be seen what prizes may be discovered in these ancient halls, or what the factions throughout the cluster may find. Meanwhile, surviving members of the Astartes kill teams [the Marshal's Mules](#/factions/marshals-mules) and [the Phyletic Vector](#/factions/phyletic-vector) extracted themselves from the wreckage and began a quiet campaign to regroup and summon their parent factions.
