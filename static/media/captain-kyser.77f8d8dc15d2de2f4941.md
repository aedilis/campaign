# Invasion of Hegesthes System

Captain Kyser is known for taking field command against the Orks of [Hegesthes](#/maps/hegesthes), embedding within one of his platoons to lead from the front. His record speaks for itself, as a recent Imperial success under his leadership saw the first breakthrough of the stalemated lines in years. After ordering a [strategic bombing](#/events/hegesthes-bombing-run) against the camp of an upcoming warboss, Kyser [led the charge](#/events/hegesthes-camp-raid) to clean up all survivors, and killed every Ork that had survived the initial attack.

&nbsp;

Kyser's Third Company became the head of a larger effort to reclaim lost territory, and they progressed further eastward and [reconquered](#/events/hegesthes-outpost-kappa-12) the overrun Outpost Kappa-12 from a warband of occupying xenos, though [a surviving weirdboy](#/characters/wortskab) escaped and spread word of a proppa skrap to be had. Now the Orks are beginning to amass with numbers not seen in years, and Kyser's greatest test yet awaits in how he will respond to this newly-organized foe.
