Kaptin Urkrod Blakkjaw was a proppa freebootin' Ork who led the initial [Bilge Squigz](#/factions/bilge-squigz) invasion of the Caldraeus Cluster in 016.M42 from his seat on the *Skrap Throne*. Unfortunately, his armada was scattered by [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox) in a battle that destroyed Blakkjaw's flagship and routed the surviving Orks into the various planets of the Hegesthes System, where they remain disunited and squabbling with each other until a proppa warboss can unite the krew once more.
