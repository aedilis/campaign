The Caldraeus System is an Imperial system within the Caldraeus Cluster. It is the regional power that controls all Imperial operations within the entirety of the sub-sector. The sector is explicitly governed by an Ecclesiarchy-aligned Administratum, which gave the organization authority as its foothold near the critical Warp anomaly.

# Background

The Caldraeus System was initially settled by humans during the Age of Technology, and was completely within Imperial control by M39, along with much of the rest of the Maelstrom Zone.

&nbsp;

The systems of the Caldraeus Cluster form a logical governing unit, and the Caldraeus System's centrality and stability made it a reasonable seat of control. To work against the potentially malign influence of the Maelstrom, the system was given to the Ecclesiarchy, and a number of Inquisitors conduct their operations from [Caldraeus](#/maps/caldraeus) as a result.
