[Kharghur](#/characters/kharghur) of the World Eaters Warband the [Blood Reavers](#/factions/blood-reavers) leads a patrol through darkness of the Starfort. Echoes sound, alerting the Lord's transhuman hearing to the presence of a foe. Kharghur's Berserkers have also heard and their mannerisms reflect the butcher nails beginning to claw into their brains. The Blood Reavers rush forward to find battle.

Astartes. Clad in black armor, the sight of them makes the Nails flare. Ten of Kharghur's legionaries are lost immediately, they won't take orders well, but their warpath will lead them to one of the four key locations to hold so Kharghur lets them go. The mortal devotees are more controlled and rush up the center. Kharghur and the other berserkers stay controlled, resisting the fire in their brains.

The Templars move forward in a disciplined line, laying down fire in controlled bursts, the Rage of Khorne keeps most of the Reavers rushing though. More Astartes take to a hold point as they leave their transport.

The butchers nails are irresistible at this point and only Kharghur through sheer will is able to retain a semblance of order. The lost berserkers blitz around the corner, laying into the transport with their chain blades, carving out chunks with adamantium teeth. The remaining legionaries butcher the newly arrived Astartes down to a single marine, Kharghur spurs his juggernaut and with a roar cracks a vital point of armor and is able to disable the vehicle.

The Templars rush forward now in range, swords bared and charge into the fighting World Eaters their blades cut down a few, but the beserkers kill more before the charges fully are effective, but lose more to the intercessors just after.

The Jakhals, without any nails, see an opening and move to take the final control point. Seeing this, Kharghur lets the Butcher's Nails envelop his mind as he sets his sight on the Marshal. With a mighty roar of BLOOD FOR THE BLOOD GOD Kharghur thunders into melee with the Marshal trampling and hacking the Astartes down before he is able to respond. All berserkers surround the remaining few Space Marines and one by one cut them down. The slaughter soothes the agony in Kharghur's mind for a moment... but as the enemy fall and the fighting ceases, even though they have fully claimed this holdout, the nails begin to bite...
