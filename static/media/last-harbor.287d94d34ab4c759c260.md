Last Harbor is a formerly Imperial world in the [Merope System](#/maps/merope-system).

# Background

The Merope System was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. However, settlement has always been sparse, and Last Harbor never had much presence to speak of.

# M42

Following the 020.M42 victory of the [Cousinry of the Broken Chain](#/factions/cousinry-of-the-broken-chain) on [Radaghast](#/maps/radaghast), members of the rebellion quickly secured the remaining planets in the system as well. Last Harbor has been governed by the Cousinry since then. Without proximate Tyranids to trigger an invasion, existence has been peaceful, as the cultists believe they achieved the freedom they fought for.
