Melee-loving boss of the Orks on Eryx along the new Chaos front. Fond of his pet squig, Nibbla.

# War on Eryx

Gorgob came up in the ranks during the long struggle against the Imperials of [Eryx](#/maps/eryx) ever since the initial Ork armada was destroyed in the Hegesthes system. He takes great pride in his mighty customized power klaw, and was delighted to find himself attacked by Chaos forces hard enough to be worthy of his and his ladz's close combat weapons. Currently locked in a brutal stalemate with the forces of the Night Lord [Vargouille](#/characters/lord-vargouille), which suits him just fine.
