> Imperial Vox Relay, 365 024.M42

Militarum Tempestus forces under their commander Garamond rapidly deploy to a strategic chokepoint, intent on halting the advance of more Orks drawn in by the fighting before they can collapse the Imperium's successful campaign of reconquest. The assembled Orks whoop and cheer in approval at the noise of the hovering Valkyrie and humming Taurox armored personnel carriers.

The blue-clad humies begin quickly. Taurox speed forward toward open lines of shooting, while hulking Ogryns claim the central no man's land and the aircraft drops off three armored Bullgryns. The ship's guns then open up on the Orks' trukk as it crunches through some ruins, but between the cover and cloud of smog, the trukk somehow emerges unscathed. A Taurox fires into Urlakk's band of boyz, but only two drop.

"We'z tuffer dan dey'z used to!" observes Urlakk proudly. "Guess dis is dere furst time seein' da power of a proppa green tide!"

The thing about humies is they're always thinking about strategy like holding territory or claiming objectives, but that suited Urlakk just fine, since this advance lined up with his strategy which he liked to call: Get Stuck In Fasta. "Charge da gitz!" he roared to his other units, and each made a beeline for a different enemy. Urlakk legged it as fast as he could for the big gits in the middle. Middle was best, after all, since you could get to the next best fight the fastest once you were done with yours, and he especially liked the Ork-sized humies that sometimes accompanied these blue humies that liked to move in trukks like Evil Sunz and to lay down dakka like Bad Moons. The trukk boyz to his right sped forward toward the armored version of the big humies, but these were no speed freaks, and they had to clamber out of their trukk before they could get stuck in properly, so Urlakk hoped there would be enemies left for him over there before they could hog them all for themselves.

Finally, the large unit to his left, where he had stuck the weirdboy that told them about this fight, became encased in wobbly green light. Urlakk admitted to himself that he had deliberately chosen not to go with these lads, since all that warp stuff seemed far too dodgy for his taste. He watched a few boyz clutch their heads, and for a moment it seemed disaster was certain, but Gork or Mork must have intervened, because they finally disappeared in a loud ZAP! as green lightning crackled outwards.

Urlakk reached the central crater with his power klaw primed and ready, seizing an abhumie in it and crushing it instantly. Around him, his boyz rained down a hail of choppa attacks on the other two. One collapsed shortly, and while the other appeared to hold on and ready a counterattack, Urlakk's painboy Dok Dreggrot leered as he stuck a horrendously unsanitary-looking needle into its exposed arm. Urlakk chose not to think about the next time he'd need medical care as the swill from Dreggrot's vial caused the abhumie to go pale and buckle. Off in the distance, he watched the weirdboy's unit materialize directly behind one of those red-robed cogboys, who had just enough time to look confused before he disappeared under a swarm of cheering Orks.

Urlakk turned his attention on the humie trukks, just as their passengers disembarked. Five measly humies each, plus another one that looked so sickly and weedy Urlakk wondered if humies had their own version of grots. The regular humies unslung their guns as the weedy one put a finger to his temple. Didn't they just see how their big dakkajet and trukks couldn't do anything against us? Urlakk thought to himself. What could a handful of gunners possibly—

And suddenly Urlakk couldn't hear himself think anymore over the noise. Shells, lasers, and plasma poured onto them from every direction, while some boyz seemed to be invisibly wrenched from the ground and tossed to splatter against buildings or fling high into the air. He looked for cover in the crater but couldn't find any in all the chaos, while Dreggrot tried desperately to press limbs back into place and staple gaping wounds shut as quickly as they appeared. He was vaguely aware of even more of the blue gits dropping in from the sky, appearing suddenly on the battlefield like some kind of Blood Axes and tearing into the weirdboy's unit, but could barely focus on the boys in front of him. He heard an explosion to his right, and realized the trukk must have been destroyed amid the blistering hail of dakka. All around him, boyz kept dropping until the only ones on their feet were himself, Dok Dreggrot, and one nob tough enough to take a major injury and stay standing.

"COME ON LADZ!" Urlakk bellowed as loudly as he could, and a few of the collapsed boyz actually did manage to stagger back to their feet, helped along by Dreggrot juicing them up with the same needle that had brought down the abhumie. It would have to do. Maybe these humies could outdakka them, but no one could outchoppa the Orks. "WAAAGH!" Urlakk howled encouragingly, as he sprinted toward the closest humie trukk and its occupants. He wrenched his power klaw into the trukk's hull while his boyz sliced and stabbed at the dakka humies, and saw the surviving trukk boyz minus their trukk had the same idea from the opposite side. Maybe these humies knew their dakka, but they were still just humies in the end, and the infantry and trukk could not stand to two squads of Orks for long. Urlakk was grateful enough for the help that he decided not to be angry when he saw the corpses of the three armored abhumies; seemed like it took a good chunk of the trukk boyz to do it, but they had taken them down.

Besides, there was plenty more fight to be had. The ladz with the weirdboy had taken almost as much of a pounding as he had, but had managed to close the gap and start chopping up the new arrivals. Based on the big banner they were carrying and silly hat one of them had, Urlakk guessed this was the humie boss, but it was so hard to tell for sure when they were all the same diminutive size. Across the battlefield, Urlakk's last surprise had revealed itself; a giant ramshackle walker had finally pistoned itself into the battle, and ripped the last humie trukk to shreds with its klaws.

Seemed like the surviving humies by the deff dread had some business in the ruins nearby, because they actually moved toward the construct and not away from it. The walker let loose with its flamer at a lucky angle and caught three out before they could make it inside, which seemed to really upset the others. Guess they needed one more for whatever they were planning, Urlakk shrugged to himself. If he had made it into a building while all his slower mates got roasted, he would have laughed and said that's what you get for mucking about. He was interrupted by the sound of pulsating air as the humie dakkajet hovered over him, and he snapped back to attention. Much as he wanted to claim the humie boss for himself, it was getting harder to see him under the sea of green, and Urlakk guessed there wouldn't be any fight left by the time he got there, so he raced the trukk boyz to the flyer. It was low enough that he thought he could scrape the bottom with his klaw, and saw a convenient upwardly-sloped rock formation on the crater he had fought in earlier. Urlakk was nothing if not cunning, and after some quick mental calculation, ran as fast as he could up the slope and leaped at the edge, power klaw arched high to catch the aircraft. He missed by a mile and faceplanted into the crater instead.

Must be too high after all, he thought as he stood up and brushed himself off, but turned around to see his ladz eagerly stabbing upward into the hull and wiring and somehow scoring hits. Hmmph. At least he was back among the bodies of his boyz from earlier and could see a few still had some life in them yet. "Back to it ladz!" he barked, and a couple more pulled themselves together and rejoined the Waaagh!

![right-1.0](10_green_tide_rises/03-01.jpg "Garamond's last stand")

The humie airship banked hard and boosted away from the fight, heading in the direction Urlakk had come from originally. He had left some grots back there to loot any good humie scrap, he remembered, and should probably go check on them at some point before the thieving gits hid all the best bits for themselves. Besides, he saw his walker peel out from the ruins and chase after the flyer, so if it was done there were no other humies left to fight. Though you never knew with these Blood Axey gits, always dropping in where you least expect them.

Scarcely had Urlakk thought that when five more did exactly that: suddenly there they were, near where the trukk and armored abhumies had gone down, scanning something and talking urgently into their wrists. Urlakk whooped and readied a charge, before remembering that because he had beaten the trukk boyz to the flyer, they were much closer. He watched in disappointment as the humies went down before he could climb back out of the rubble and the humie dakkajet gained altitude and disappeared.

Oh well, he thought to himself as he surveyed the carnage across the now-quiet battlefield. Plenty more where that came from.
