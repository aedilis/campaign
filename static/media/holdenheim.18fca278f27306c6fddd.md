Holdenheim is an Imperial-aligned planet in the [Holdenheim System](#/maps/holdenheim-system).

# Background

Holdenheim was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence here.

&nbsp;

At some point in its history, St. Holden played a significant role, and the planet was renamed for them.

# M42

Black Templars of the [Revenant Crusade](#/factions/revenant-crusade) began scouting the planet after freeing their ship from the [Mournful Sepulchre](#/maps/mournful-sepulchre). They intervened at St. Holden's Hive to break an Ork siege that threatened to overwhelm the defenders of the local guard.
