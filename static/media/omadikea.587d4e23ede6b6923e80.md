Omadikea is a formerly Imperial world in the [Omadikea System](#/maps/omadikea-system).

# Background

Omadikea was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence here.

# M42

After the arrival of the [Daybreak Protectorate](#/factions/daybreak-protectorate) and its union with the Omadikea System, Omadikea became the seat of the new government.
