The *Umbra Mortis* is a former knight of [Telemane](#/maps/telemane) that has fallen to corruption and now serves [Zaldrotha Voidrender](#/characters/zaldrotha-voidrender).

# The Telemane War

The *Umbra Mortis* was the first to fall to Chaos corruption amidst the [initial invasion](#/events/defense-of-telemane) in 023.M42. In that battle, it was destroyed by the loyalist [*Sentinel of Duty*](#/characters/sentinel-of-duty), and appeared to have been defeated. However, Warp machinations led to its reconstitution, and it led the traitors of House Mordgeir in the [battle of Hive Aspidopolis](#/events/battle-of-hive-aspidopolis) that led to the Night Lords' conquest of the planet.
