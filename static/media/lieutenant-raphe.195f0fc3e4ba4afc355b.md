Decorated lieutenant with the [317th Hegesthean Regiment](#/factions/hegesthean-dusters) known for his willingness to engage in whatever combat is necessary for the success of the mission.

## Invasion of Hegesthes System

Lieutenant Raphe has command of the Fourth Platoon under the Third Company's [Captain Kyser](#/characters/captain-kyser), and the two officers have a strong relationship when deployed on the battlefield. Raphe was recently commended for heroism after a [successful attack](#/events/hegesthes-camp-raid) on an Ork camp in which he interrupted an Ork biker's charge with a swift strike of his power sword, breaking his wrist but killing the attacker before he could engage and almost assuredly saving the life of his regimental standard-bearer.
