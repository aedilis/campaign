Radaghast is a formerly Imperial moon in the [Merope System](#/maps/merope-system).

# Background

The Merope System was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. Radaghast has always been the population center of the system, given its easy access to the valuable gases of [Merope](#/maps/merope).

&nbsp;

Some time before the end of M41, the Imperium set up a penal colony on Radaghast that compelled its prisoners to mine Merope and ship its product to the rest of the cluster.

# M42

Following the 020.M42 victory of the [Cousinry of the Broken Chain](#/factions/cousinry-of-the-broken-chain), members of the rebellion quickly secured the remaining planets in the system as well. Radaghast has been governed by the Cousinry since then. Without proximate Tyranids to trigger an invasion, existence has been peaceful, as the cultists believe they achieved the freedom they fought for.
