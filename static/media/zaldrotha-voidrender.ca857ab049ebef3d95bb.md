Admiral Zaldrotha Voidrender is the Night Lords Warmaster with command of *The Last Breath*. As the leader of the Chaos incursion into Caldrean space, he currently directs the traitor Astartes as they expand their fell presence from their base at Belgatha.

## Invasion

The Voidrender entered the Caldraeus Cluster with a [wildly successful first strike](#/events/doom-fleet-invades) on the [unsuspecting defenders](#/factions/fleet-of-the-amber-cloud). After destroying six Imperial ships and routing the surviving fleet commander, [Admiral Umbranox](#/characters/otto-umbranox), Zaldrotha claimed uncontested control of the space around the industrial world [Belgatha](#/maps/belgatha), beginning planetfall and preparation of a base from which to enter the second phase of his legion's invasion.

&nbsp;

As he led the dominance of Belgatha, he ordered his lieutenants to expand their foothold in the cluster; one lieutenant led an [initial invasion](#/events/knights-vs-chaos) of the planet [Eryx](#/maps/eryx) (though that was quickly rebuffed after the planet had time to prepare with Umbranox's quick raising of defenses), while [Lord Malithor](#/characters/lord-malithor) invaded and [conquered](#/events/naxos-besieged) the stations orbiting the dead world of [Naxos](#/maps/naxos). Amidst all this, Zaldrotha himself turned his attention to the final Imperial holdout in the Belgatha System.

## The Telemane War

The feudal world of [Telemane](#/maps/telemane), home to a number of great Knight Houses, drew Zaldrotha's attention as prime grounds for corruption. However, his momentum was finally halted, as the well-prepared Imperial defenders [held the line](#/events/defense-of-telemane) against his initial assault. Seeing that he would struggle to match their numbers and conquer the planet by traditional means, Zaldrotha ordered the undertaking of a [long campaign of subterfuge](#/events/ashes-of-faith) that would weaken defenses and destroy the loyalist morale. While agents of the Inquisition were ultimately able to stop a ritual that would have plunged Telemane into Warp influence, the long campaign had captured enough territory and spread enough corruption that the Voidrender was able to claim his greatest prize yet: control of the Knightly House of Mordgeir.

&nbsp;

With support of the *Umbra Mortis* along with its corrupted retainers, the Night Lords [conquered Hive Aspidopolis](#/events/battle-of-hive-aspidopolis), center of remaining Imperial control, in an extremely close battle. The strong resistance put up by its defenders bought enough time for the Imperium to evacuate key war machines and personnel, but the vacuum left behind has allowed the Night Lords Warmaster to bring the rest of Telemane nearly under his control, with pockets of loyalist PDF and Knights remaining to conduct a war of attrition. Where the Warmaster will turn his attention next is currently unknown.
