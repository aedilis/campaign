A breakaway coalition of xenos and human allies maintains a very limited foothold within the cluster. The enclave's early success can be largely attributed to the complete isolation from the wider Imperium that its planets have felt, sparing them the wrath of the Emperor's anti-xenos loyalists. While the protectorate slowly grows in strength, it is only a matter of time before it is noticed, and despite the best efforts of its diplomats, a reckoning with the regional power is widely expected.

# Background

Knowledge of the Farsight Enclaves is widespread among the T'au, though almost entirely as a cautionary tale of a fallen hero whom the Ethereals teach others to leave behind in shame. However, some take inspiration from Commander Farsight's breakaway empire, and wonder if the Greater Good is best served by the kind of equality among castes that his domain espouses.

&nbsp;

The doomed Fourth Sphere of Expansion of 999.M41, tragic as it was, offered a rare opportunity to enact this model, far from the oversight of the territory of the T'au Empire and its Ethereals. When the colonist ships were scattered in the Warp by the experimental slipstream module, the exact path each vessel took was unpredictable. The fleet of Commander Daybreak was buffeted along a Warp current until it emerged from a natural opening connected to the maelstroms around the Caldraeus Cluster. While the colonists struggled mightily to remain with their comrades under the expansion's leader Commander Surestrike, they were fortunate to be separated, as they escaped the madness that befell the others trapped in this unknown part of reality.

&nbsp;

So it was that the forces under Daybreak emerged, safe from the horrors of the Warp but separated from the rest of T'au civilization, as the natural eddy of Warp current they followed out soon shifted, with no connection to the Startide Nexus of the Sept Worlds or to the Nem'yar Atoll of the Chalnath Expanse, where the Fourth Sphere survivors would eventually and mysteriously reappear. Daybreak's initial survey revealed enough survivors and supplies that their Water Caste diplomats assessed they could offer resources and stability to nearby worlds. The pressure to do so was mounting, however, as the survivors could not sustain themselves aboard the ships indefinitely.

&nbsp;

Daybreak hailed from Ko'sei Sept, close with Farsight's origins in Vior'la, and perhaps this was a source of some of her sympathies for him. Whatever sympathies there were had been further compounded by her resentment of the Ethereals, who overruled the Earth Caste's objections to deploy this experimental FTL prototype long before sufficient study of the Imperial technology could be made. Any Ethereals that had accompanied them perished upon exposure to the Immaterium, and the soon-to-be Protectorate decided to invite all worlds they encountered to a new society in the model of Farsight's grand experiment.

&nbsp;

So it was that the coalition began their task, not under the authority of an Ethereal, but united by an elemental council aboard the Daybreak's flagship *The Ardent Purpose*: Shas'O Ko'sei K'yen Daybreak, Por'O Uvash'a the Striving Dawn, Kor'O Ho'sen the Sustaining Rain, and Fio'O D'tano the Eternal Sower.

# Success in Omadikea
    
The turbulent Warp storms had not spared the settled planets of the region. While the entirety of the Caldraeus Cluster has long been subjected to unusual Warp activity, the [system of Omadikea](#/maps/omadikea-system) had found itself cut off from all contact with the Imperium for decades. It was with this context, of a generation of humans for whom the Imperium was a little-understood and distant power and which offered them no aid against encroaching raiders from the Maelstrom, that the planetary government received the first Water Caste diplomats with hesitant but receptive minds. When the firepower of the T'au fleet turned away pirates and the heavy larders intended for early colonists relieved a small planetary famine, the humans of Omadikea came around to the value of cooperation and formally entered the newly formed Daybreak Protectorate.

&nbsp;

Daybreak set up her headquarters on the [planet Omadikea](#/maps/omadikea) and immediately set to work updating the Imperial infrastructure to a standard fit for her Gue'vesa allies. This was reasonably straightforward, given the ships were specifically equipped with tools and resources meant to bring a world up to the standards of a thriving T'au colony. Meanwhile, the [jungle world Alektor](#/maps/alektor) was a perfect target for her Kroot allies, since it resembled their homeworld Pech and human expansion was limited due to the heavy jungle biome throughout the planet. Over a decade passed of growth and prosperity, further solidifying the loyalty of the humans and giving the upstart Commander some governing experience of her own.

# Expanding the Protectorate

Water Caste diplomats of the Striving Dawn and spies of the Radiant Path wasted little time in sending emissaries to nearby systems, attempting secret meetings at first to avoid the wrath of the xenophobic Imperium, aided by ships from the Kor'vattra fleet admiral. It's possible that these meetings played a role in the decision of the Planetary Governor of [Kathikos](#/maps/kathikos) in an [adjacent system](#/maps/kathikos-system) to withdraw from the Imperium and declare independence. The Water Caste has pressed the government on the advantages of an alliance, and offered resources to resist the incoming Imperial response that will attempt to quash the rebellion. The governor believes these attempts to be useful are the first step to a takeover, but in the face of the Imperial response has accepted T'au military resources without yet accepting integration. Por'el Calmstride, the Lucent Seeker, remains in the governor's court for now, serving as a liaison as the planet braces for war.

&nbsp;

Meanwhile, in the Kor'vattra orbiting the planet, cadres prepare for mobilization: Rising Dawn Cadre, Blazing Sun Cadre, Thunderstrike Cadre, Stormwind Cadre stand by for deployment against Imperial aggression.
