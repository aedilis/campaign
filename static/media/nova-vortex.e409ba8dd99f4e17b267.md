Vespid auxiliaries accompanied the Daybreak Protectorate to the Caldraeus Cluster and remain on call for any operations that require their skills. The Radiant Hunter strain, recognizable by their dark carapaces and orange wings, compose the bulk of these auxiliaries, and follow the typical pattern of formations guided by a link between a Vespid Strain Leader and a T'au Fire Warrior.

# Background

Formation Nova Vortex is organized around the joint direction of Shas'ui Vior'la Ko'ryr and the Strain Leader designated NV-One for purposes of field communications.

# Caldraeus

While the Daybreak Protectorate does not seek war with the wider Imperium, it intends to defend the territory of its gue'la allies and expand to as many other human worlds as it can. In pursuit of this goal, they have sought the strategic assets available on the newly arrived Space Hulk, hoping to use its communications array to cut through the ever-present noise of the Warp in the sector. As an initial effort to understand the territory better and aggressively advance on this territory, Formation Nova Vortex was dispatched to an exploration of the Void Phantom, along with the Kyrek Kinband. Stationed in the so-called High Flyers Suite, Ko'ryr directs the formation through the maze-like structure as best as he can.
