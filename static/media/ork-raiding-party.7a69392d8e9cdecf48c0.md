*Ever since the first fight with the bugeyez, Krogskull Gobrekka knew that there had to be a way to get to wherever they came from and give them a proppa punch-up, and he was determined to get there. A team of oddboyz, led by his big mek Badratchit, had been scrapping dutifully with the crashed humie tech, occasionally producing some loud zaps and blips that resulted in new pockets of bugeyez appearing nearby. This was always greeted with a roar of approval from the assembled boyz, who got stuck in with gusto. The bugeyez liked to get in close so it was always a proppa scrap, although a few of the gits always seemed to peel off and make a beeline for the trees and disappear while the Orks were distracted, but that was probably fine.*

*Finally, today was the auspicious day when Badratchit told the boss he had successfully tinkered with the gubbins just right and they were ready to tellyport from their end to wherever all the fun was on the other end and really get the fun started. First Badratchit rolled a bunch of squigs into the chamber and tellyported them away, but after they realized the squigs wouldn’t be able to report back, they stuffed a bunch of protesting grots into the chamber and tellyported them. Then they realized they had no way to tellyport the grots back from their end anyway, so said zog it and loaded into the chamber themselves.*

*With a zap of light blue humie lighting with some orky green sparking that Badratchit had added just to make the whole process a little more proppa, Gobrekka’s boarding party disappeared from Squig Run.*

*A short stretch of confusing color and jarring acceleration later, they found themselves in a dark, vast room, silent but for some low whirring, with narrow corridors jutting outward into Mork knows where. They were surrounded on all sides with ancient metal panels that matched the scrap that had fallen across Squig Run all of a sudden one day and kicked off a spree of looting and scrapping amongst the resident greenskins. Gobrekka’s squigs bounded around the metal floor of the chamber with some bugeye bits in their mouths, while grots clung to the walls and dangled from the ceiling doing their best to avoid them. This must be the place, but what was it?*

*Badratchit headed straight for a series of consoles across the platform. “Lemme tinker wiv dis fer a bit, boss,” he told Gobrekka. “Gotta figure out ‘ow ta control it from dis end and mebbe figure out where dis iz.”*

*That sounded dead boring so Gobrekka grunted his permission and stode off the platform and toward the largest bolthead leading out. There had to be some good gits to fight while his mek mucked about, and he intended to find them.*

*He didn't have to go far.*
