Tybar Primus, often called just Tybar locally and known as Smashyard to the Orks, is an Ork-held planet in the [Tybar System](#/maps/tybar-system).

# Background

The Tybar System appears to have been within the domain of an ancient Necron dynasty centered on [Kalymnia](#/maps/kalymnia). Little is known about what became of this dynasty, and whether Tybar Primus would have been a significant location to them.

# M41

In the 700s M41, as the nearby Hybraxis Nebula of the Maelstrom Zone faced the threat of a large Ork Waaagh!, xenos pillagers spilled into the Caldraeus Cluster as well. Though the Orks were eventually defeated, they had thoroughly overrun the Tybar, [Serres](#/maps/serres-system), and [Kalymnia](#/maps/kalymnia-system) Systems, and the Imperium strategically withdrew from all three. Tybar Primus was the most keenly felt of these losses, as a relic world with valuable religious artifacts and technology, but the threat of the Maelstrom prevented resources from being spent to reclaim the planet quickly.
