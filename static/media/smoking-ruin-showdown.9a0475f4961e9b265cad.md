Amidst the ruins and scrap yards of [Tybar](#/maps/tybar-secundus) the [Phyletic Vector](#/factions/phyletic-vector) and [Marshal's Mules](#/factions/marshals-mules) pick their way towards a smoking ruin holding a key data cache.

After [successfully infiltrating](#/events/discovery-templar-stealth-offensive) the ork encampment the Mules made a direct advance towards the cache, while the Phyletic Vector had to make a more roundabout journey to not draw the full attention of the ork horde after [failing to follow the path beginning in the tunnels of a landing pad](#/events/discovery-death-guard-infiltration).

Arriving first to the zone, Jan deftly scaled the derelict Vox relay tower and, peering through the recently acquired relic scope, he sighted the plasma gunner Malignus despite the billowing smoke and intervening ruins. Unleashing a blistering volley of fire Jan's bolts tore into the plague marine, mass reactive shells erupting within the decaying marine. But the warp infused astartes barely broke stride as its flesh was torn asunder, striding in as ichor oozed and spilled. Malignus, paying no heed to his wounds lumbered forward, leveled his plasma gun, disengaging the safety stud, orange heat shone from the coils as a burst of super heated plasma split the air, striking the veteran Jan directly, ceramite sloughed like wet mud and the Intercessor tumbled from his perch, fatality wounded, Jan was out of the fight.

Tarcuin makes haste and shifts rubble aside from a blinking auspex marker, and discovers the data cache, with the asset discovered, Tarcuin sets to defend to ensure the traitors are unable to seize the intel. [Myasum Clostrid](#/characters/myasum-clostrid) makes speed towards the ruin also searching a chirping auspex node but finds only some worthless piece of ruined machine.

Cuthbert advanced up the side, preparing a krak grenade with intent to avenge Jan. Operatives on both sides push forward and begin to envelop the ruins.

Seizing the initiative, Cuthbert braces against an embankment and aims at the wounded Malignus. His underslung grenade launcher barked as the kraken round buried itself in the wounds of Malignus and the armor rending detonation hurled the plague marine backwards collapsing in a heap. Festas bearing the icon of Nurgle sought to kill Cuthbert but in cover, he suffered only a few wounds as he ducked to reload another kraken round. Ludolf makes the move to breach the ruined bunker and confirms no data is left behind. Hearing the Templar nearby, Myasum Clostrid moves to scale the smoking building, thumb the safety stud off on his pistol. The smoke burns clear in a short tunnel as the supercharged shot burns into Ludolf dropping him. The Mules see the Plague Champion alone and make to cut the head off the snake, Tarcuin scales the bunker just enough to lob a krak grenade, Albrecht climbs the Vox relay vantage and lays covering fire into the ruin, giving time for [Engelbert](#/characters/sergeant-engelbert) to redirect, and engulf Clostrid in holy fire. As the flames die, explosions fade, and bolt rounds quiet, Myasum Clostrid stands, the gifts of the Lord of Decay glistening beneath the scorched flesh and ceramite.

With a duel of champions imminent Myasum Clostrid once more charges his plasma pistol, the heat building, curling the smoke around it, the flash of heat streaks towards Sergeant Engelbert, but it is but a glancing blow, and wounding but not injuring the Sergeant. Clostrid decides not to risk the wrath of a thunderhammer considering how much damage he sustained, and falls back to be covered by Thrax and Gulchor. Englebert sees his prey retreating and doesn't wish to let his prize get away so easily, rushing to the edge of the bunker he washes the Plague Champion with his hand flamer to finish the kill, but once more it is not enough. Recognizing his exposure Engelbert moves to the cover of the smoke to avoid retaliation fire.

Festas once again tries to kill Cuthbert but fails again, Cuthbert unleashes a hail of devastation, but the tenacity of Festas is astonishing, withstanding volley fire of kraken rounds, and only falling finally after repeated and focused attacks from most of the remaining Mules.

Sergeant Engelbert spots the heretic Pustus approaching and charges. Plummeting down from the ruin like an avenging comet, thunderhammer crackling with lightning, he caves in the breastplate of the death gust then spins the hammer around for a lighting fast respite, crashing into the helm of the plague marine, stumbling off balance Pustus lashes out with a blade only scratching the sergeant before the hammer comes again. Striking down in two handed blow like a smith at a forge, Engelbert crushes the form of Pustus with a mighty blow that ceases all movement of his foe.

Opposite the sergeant Gulchor and Thrax make a combined effort of fire to fell the assault intercessors Tarcuin. Cutherbert dashed to secure the data cache, and Albrecht stand vigilant on Overwatch high above.

Myasum Clostrid turns and stalks toward Sergeant Engelbert, now exposed. Thumbing off the safety stud he overloads the generator of his pistols. As he walks forward Myasum Clostrid calls forth an oath to the Grandfather of Plagues, a dark pact forming on his blistering and bleeding lips, raising his pistol, vision blinks blind white as the weapons explodes in a boom of actinic fury. The plasma bolt screams out towards Englebert in the same moment the gun detonated washing Myasum Clostrid in the heat of a contained star. Struck down simultaneously Engelberts falling determination to fire one last salvo burns through empty air as the plague champion falls to his own weapon, the faint chortling of neverborn voices echoing...

As both champion lie on the field, burned and dying the battlefield starts to quiet as both sides carry off their casualties. They both held the territory they needed but the Marshal's Mules walk away with the data cache.
