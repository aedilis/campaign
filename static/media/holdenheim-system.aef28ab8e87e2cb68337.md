The Holdenheim System takes its name from its most notable planet, the Imperial world of [Holdenheim](#/maps/holdenheim).

# Background

The Holdenheim System was initially settled in the M30s and has been continuously occupied by humans ever since. It is unknown if there was previous Xenos presence on any of its worlds.

&nbsp;

Worlds are defended by local PDF and potentially regiments from elsewhere in the cluster.
